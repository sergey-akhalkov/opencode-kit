## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Delivery trajectory routing SHALL be precise and proportional

The loaded global authority, canonical OpenSpec propose/apply/archive skills and
commands, project templates, catalogs, and discovery metadata SHALL expose one compact
route for explicit Delivery Horizons. Successful horizon-linked archive SHALL invoke a
cheap signal; only a current material trigger SHALL load the complete
`roadmap-delivery-trajectory` body; and only the same horizon's next substantial
dependent investment SHALL require the current disposition.

The trajectory skill description SHALL name roadmap or phase delivery trajectory,
post-archive forecast/bottleneck/unit-of-work triggers, and stay-quiet boundaries for
ordinary change retrospectives, cohesive local work, changed-code review, focused
complexity assessment, next-step recommendation, exhaustive audit, and campaign
execution. No new Practice Owner or reviewer-owned disposition SHALL be registered.

#### Scenario: Ordinary archive has no explicit horizon

- **WHEN** a completed change is legacy-unlinked or declares `Delivery Horizon: none`
- **THEN** archive reports trajectory `not-applicable` without loading deep review
- **AND** no global workflow infers a roadmap from task, capability, file, or archive
  names.

#### Scenario: Linked archive has a material unit-of-work trigger

- **WHEN** compact current evidence ties repeated item-specific work to one shared-owner
  mechanism and the same horizon outcome
- **THEN** main loads the on-demand trajectory skill exactly once for the current tuple
- **AND** adjacent complexity, audit, next-step, campaign, or reviewer workflows are not
  selected as substitute owners.

### Requirement: Trajectory reflection SHALL not restore final-retrospective ceremony

Loaded propose, apply, archive, compaction, handoff, and trajectory instructions SHALL
keep successful product completion and optional process reflection separate. They SHALL
NOT create a final-history task, analyze every change, append improvements to a completed
change, require a six-cell retrospective matrix, or make a no-trigger signal durable.
Only a current material horizon trigger MAY create one immutable review receipt and one
ordinary future successor or evidence slice.

#### Scenario: No material trigger exists

- **WHEN** accepted scope and archive are complete and the cheap trajectory signal is
  `none`
- **THEN** the workflow stops trajectory processing without a report, receipt, task, or
  successor
- **AND** normal completion and next-step routing remain available.

#### Scenario: Triggered review chooses future work

- **WHEN** one current review admits an outcome-preserving successor
- **THEN** that work is represented through ordinary new OpenSpec artifacts and proof
  rather than tasks appended to the archived change
- **AND** review completion itself supplies no product, implementation, or archive credit.

### Requirement: Loaded trajectory behavior SHALL be proven at actual entrypoints

The maintained instruction proof SHALL exercise actual configured propose, complete-
archive, signal, trajectory-skill, and successor-planning entrypoints in disposable
projects under matched model, profile, permissions, environment, horizon, accepted
outcome, archive result, and initial state. Hard archive, outcome, owner-boundary,
privacy, no-duplicate, effect, diagnostics, and cleanup oracles SHALL pass before any
workflow-friction or acceleration claim.

Provider-free schema/fixture checks and static markers SHALL support but SHALL NOT
replace loaded semantic behavior. A missing skill/helper in the selected profile SHALL
report the exact capability unavailable and SHALL NOT approximate deep review from an
adjacent skill or another source.

#### Scenario: Candidate preserves archive and owner boundary

- **WHEN** matched candidate scenarios exercise no-trigger, outcome-preserving replan,
  and quality-weakening options
- **THEN** archive remains terminal, exactly one valid successor appears only in the
  replan case, and the weaker option remains owner-required without mutation
- **AND** every session, process, fixture, and writer reaches terminal cleanup.

#### Scenario: Profile omits trajectory capability

- **WHEN** a custom profile lacks the trajectory skill or exact helper closure
- **THEN** loaded routing reports trajectory capability unavailable for the linked
  horizon
- **AND** it does not load complexity, next-step, audit, campaign, or a guessed global
  source as a semantic fallback.
