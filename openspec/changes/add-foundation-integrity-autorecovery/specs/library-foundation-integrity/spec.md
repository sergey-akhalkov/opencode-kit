## Purpose

Defines project-foundation identity review and autonomous recovery so locally valid work cannot silently advance a current outcome on mismatched workload, profile, environment, or oracle foundations.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Material foundation bindings receive one event-triggered integrity review
The runtime SHALL treat a foundation binding as the current relationship among an
accepted product outcome or current-oracle identity and at least one independently
named representative workload/population, machine or language profile,
configuration, environment, or observation-oracle identity. Main SHALL invoke the
registered `foundation-integrity` Practice Owner before further dependent investment
when current work binds or rebinds at least two such identities and current evidence
also shows one of the following: a durable current identity differs; a historical,
cross-profile, or other-estate identity is used as the current representative path;
or two or more locally passing evidence lanes are about to compose into delivery
progress on that binding.

Generic possibility that a task could affect quality, a single-identity local defect,
an aligned binding, or historical evidence explicitly retained below the current
claim SHALL NOT trigger the owner. Ordinary Small exact-case work with no material
foundation binding SHALL remain a negative control. Trigger applicability remains a
reviewed semantic judgment; deterministic tooling MUST NOT infer it from keywords,
file names, counts, similarity, or scores.

#### Scenario: Historical workload is bound to a different current oracle
- **WHEN** durable current evidence binds outcome A to profile/configuration/oracle A and a current phase change binds a historical workload from profile/configuration B as outcome A's representative workload
- **THEN** main invokes only the `foundation-integrity` Practice Owner before further dependent proof or implementation investment
- **AND** locally passing transport, timing, compilation, or substitution evidence does not suppress the trigger.

#### Scenario: Historical evidence remains explicitly historical
- **WHEN** a change retains a foreign-profile workload only as historical, component, or regression evidence and binds the current outcome to the matching current workload/profile/oracle identities
- **THEN** the foundation-integrity review is not required solely because the historical identity is referenced
- **AND** its existing narrower evidence remains available.

#### Scenario: Ordinary Small work has no foundation event
- **WHEN** one bounded exact-case local change neither binds nor rebinds a representative workload, profile, configuration, environment, or oracle
- **THEN** no foundation-integrity owner or recovery procedure is launched
- **AND** ordinary proof and validation remain sufficient.

### Requirement: Foundation findings are evidence-backed and material
The foundation-integrity reviewer SHALL be fresh, read-only, non-authorizing, and
non-orchestrating. It SHALL compare current accepted outcome and foundation identities
against directly readable current source, specifications, decisions, runtime evidence,
claim records, and supplied reviewer results. It SHALL return the common Practice
Owner fields, one foundation-relation matrix, and only risk rows that identify a
reachable current scenario, concrete contradiction or material unknown, affected
outcome or non-deferrable invariant, business consequence, evidence-backed
likelihood or `unknown`, confidence, and the smallest falsifying observation when
feasible.

The reviewer SHALL preserve useful narrower evidence and MAY return
`no-material-finding`, `not-applicable`, or `unknown`. Style, optional architecture
improvement, future extensibility, unrelated maintainability debt, non-critical test
polish, and speculative risk MUST NOT be represented as a foundation recovery
finding. The reviewer SHALL NOT create an implementation plan, choose product
semantics, mutate artifacts, dispatch another agent, or authorize recovery.

#### Scenario: Local evidence is valid below the current outcome
- **WHEN** a current workload/profile/oracle contradiction is evidenced but completed local proof remains valid for exact bytes on the exercised path
- **THEN** the reviewer reports the current foundation risk and the narrower valid evidence ceiling separately
- **AND** it does not erase the local result or approve a correction.

#### Scenario: Only architecture polish is available
- **WHEN** the inspected binding is current and aligned and the only observation is that a module split, abstraction, naming scheme, or additional future test could be cleaner
- **THEN** the reviewer returns no foundation recovery finding for that observation
- **AND** it does not create a backlog or refer optional polish as required recovery work.

### Requirement: Main autonomously recovers confirmed foundation defects
After a material finding, main SHALL independently reproduce or falsify the exact
foundation contradiction before semantic mutation. If main falsifies the finding,
it SHALL record the conflicting evidence and continue without creating recovery
work or requesting an equivalent re-review. If main confirms the finding and the
accepted outcome plus current safe evidence uniquely determine a correction, main
SHALL execute the on-demand foundation recovery procedure without asking for
approval of the plan, specification, tasks, or continuation.

Recovery SHALL correct the smallest current foundation relationship, preserve the
original accepted outcome, create or update the smallest project-native planning and
claim surfaces, narrow invalidated evidence rather than discard it, and continue the
original work on the corrected path. For an OpenSpec project, recovery SHALL create
or update active change artifacts and deltas through existing ownership rules. For a
project without OpenSpec, recovery SHALL use its current project-native planning
surface and MUST NOT invent an OpenSpec repository merely to satisfy this workflow.

#### Scenario: Current evidence uniquely determines the correction
- **WHEN** main reproduces that the selected representative workload belongs to a historical profile while the accepted outcome and current oracle identify one matching current workload estate
- **THEN** main autonomously rebinds the affected planning and claim path to the matching current identities
- **AND** no owner approval is requested for the corrective artifacts or task ordering.

#### Scenario: Reviewer finding is falsified
- **WHEN** direct current source, specification, or runtime evidence proves that the supposedly mismatched workload and oracle are intentionally the same accepted foundation identity
- **THEN** main creates no recovery change and records the finding as falsified for that candidate and hypothesis
- **AND** the same unchanged finding does not trigger another review.

#### Scenario: Product identity remains genuinely ambiguous
- **WHEN** safe evidence cannot distinguish two materially different current product identities and choosing one changes protected product, public API, persisted-data, security, privacy, authorization, legal, or policy meaning
- **THEN** only that identity choice remains owner-bound after every safe discriminating route is exhausted
- **AND** unrelated work continues without treating reviewer evidence as authority.

### Requirement: Confirmed invalidation propagates only to dependent current work
Recovery SHALL inventory every active change, including complete but unarchived
changes, every current canonical specification, and every active claim record that
can materially depend on the invalidated foundation relation. An artifact is
dependent only when it names the invalid identity as a current oracle,
configuration, profile, representative workload/population, production/comparison
path, or claim environment, or when its completed evidence rows bind that identity
as current. A historical-only reference is not dependence.

Main SHALL classify the complete current inventory before mutation, update only
dependent artifacts, serialize writes, and preserve unrelated active work. A
canonical OpenSpec requirement SHALL be corrected through an active delta rather
than an agent-driven direct main-spec edit. Overlapping dependent owners SHALL use
the existing dependency/ownership-transfer contract; they MUST NOT mutate the same
surface concurrently.

#### Scenario: One of two active changes depends on the invalid identity
- **WHEN** two active changes are inventoried and only one treats the invalid profile as the current representative path
- **THEN** recovery updates only that dependent change and any current canonical requirement or claim that asserts the same relation
- **AND** the unrelated change retains its outcome, scope, tasks, and evidence unchanged.

#### Scenario: Two dependent changes share an owner
- **WHEN** two dependent active changes would modify the same capability or owned artifact
- **THEN** main declares an acyclic dependency or ownership transfer and corrects them serially
- **AND** no concurrent or overlapping mutation is authorized by the foundation finding.

### Requirement: Recovery preserves evidence and terminates without polishing loops
Every confirmed mismatch SHALL receive one stable incident identity bound to the
exact foundation relation, candidate, and decision-changing evidence. Its lifecycle
SHALL be `observed` followed by `falsified`, `owner-boundary`, or
`confirmed -> correcting -> swept -> re-reviewed -> closed`. Deterministic tooling
MAY validate explicit incident identities and state transitions but MUST NOT infer
the relation, materiality, correction, or closure.

Archives and immutable evidence SHALL remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Current active
claims MAY reference prior results only with their truthful historical,
cross-profile, component, regression, or other narrower ceiling. Recovery SHALL
reopen or add only tasks directly required by the corrected accepted outcome and
MUST NOT create work for non-critical findings, optional polish, future scope, or
unrelated debt.

Main SHALL obtain at most one fresh foundation-integrity review for each corrected
candidate. The same finding on the same candidate and evidence SHALL NOT create a
new incident, recovery change, or equivalent verdict-seeking review. A closed
incident SHALL remain closed unless a new candidate identity or new decision-
changing evidence establishes a distinct reachable foundation relation.

#### Scenario: Archived result used the old foundation
- **WHEN** a confirmed recovery reclassifies an archived result from current-phase evidence to historical cross-profile evidence
- **THEN** the archived files remain byte-identical and current active records state the narrower ceiling
- **AND** historical wording does not reopen the incident during corrected-candidate review.

#### Scenario: Corrected candidate receives one fresh review
- **WHEN** recovery reaches `swept` with all dependent active artifacts dispositioned
- **THEN** main launches one fresh review attributed to that corrected candidate
- **AND** `no-material-finding` closes the incident while a repeated unchanged finding cannot mint a successor recovery episode.

#### Scenario: Non-critical observation survives recovery review
- **WHEN** corrected-candidate review reports only optional architecture, maintainability, test-polish, or future-scope observations
- **THEN** the foundation incident closes without creating another change, task, or blocker
- **AND** those observations remain non-critical limitations or are omitted when they provide no current value.
