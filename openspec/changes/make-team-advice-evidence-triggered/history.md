# Strategy History

## 2026-08-31 - Replace universal advice without deleting the advisor

- **Objective:** Remove routine serial advisor cost while preserving useful team selection.
- **Approach:** Compare deleting the advisor, keeping universal advice with `main-alone`, and activating advice only for concrete selection uncertainty.
- **Evidence:** The advisor has unique value for competing routes, unavailable capabilities, independent evidence, and isolation boundaries, but a cached or repeated `main-alone` still pays a model call for known direct work.
- **Outcome:** Retained the advisor and selected evidence-triggered activation.
- **Reason:** Conditional activation removes cost from the direct path without losing the existing bounded decision owner.
- **Do-Not-Repeat Condition:** Do not remove the advisor merely because one direct scenario does not need it, and do not preserve universal calls as a cache problem.
- **Evidence-Based Retry Condition:** Reconsider only if current installed evidence proves the catalog/advisor adds no unique value across every maintained positive member.

## 2026-08-31 - Reject circular self-exemption

- **Objective:** Define a direct route that cannot be satisfied by the same unsupported model judgment it is meant to govern.
- **Approach:** Compare "main decides no advisor is useful" with a conjunction of current owner, route, proof, capability, independent-evidence, and isolation facts.
- **Evidence:** The combined SOSC plan's self-exemption had no falsifiable boundary; a model could skip advice by asserting its own conclusion. Maintained fixtures can supply and evaluate explicit current facts without deterministic semantic inference.
- **Outcome:** Selected a seven-fact complete direct predicate and one-fact-unknown positive controls.
- **Reason:** Explicit facts make the routing outcome falsifiable while leaving semantic judgment with main/advisor rather than helper code.
- **Do-Not-Repeat Condition:** Do not use task size, reversibility, confidence, or "no unique value" prose as a sufficient direct predicate.
- **Evidence-Based Retry Condition:** Revise the predicate only if current installed failures identify a necessary selection fact not represented by the seven owners.

## 2026-08-31 - Split team routing from OpenSpec artifact contracts

- **Objective:** Give advisor routing one cohesive owner and proof population.
- **Approach:** Compare retaining advisor semantics inside `streamline-ordinary-small-closure` with a separate change ordered after the artifact-contract correction.
- **Evidence:** Team Advice owns loaded main, advisor/catalog, STA-001, and continuity behavior; compact/full risk metadata owns OpenSpec gates and different failure consequences. The combined design was both fail-open and ownership-heavy.
- **Outcome:** Created `make-team-advice-evidence-triggered` after SOSC and moved the three advisor-related capability deltas here.
- **Reason:** Artifact form, risk class, and team selection are independent decisions and should not authorize one another.
- **Do-Not-Repeat Condition:** Do not reintroduce OpenSpec artifact-profile semantics into Team Advice or use direct routing as a risk/profile exemption.
- **Evidence-Based Retry Condition:** Reconsider only if current implementation evidence proves the two changes cannot preserve their accepted routes independently.
