## Why

A consumer project completed a roadmap phase by composing exhaustive offline evidence from one implementation path with representative real-system evidence from another path. Every declared task and validation gate passed, but the phase-wide claim exceeded the evidence population and real observation boundary. The kit needs a universal claim-evidence closure contract so representative proof can establish a working lane without being promoted into unsupported corpus-wide, compatibility, safety, or milestone completion claims.

A current loaded-session baseline given the mismatch as one explicit compact decision correctly refused the broad claim. The reproduced gap is therefore not "the model always answers this question incorrectly"; it is that long-lived artifact composition, archive, and completion paths have no structured closure owner and can validate a weaker contract without rechecking the original claim. Candidate evidence must retain the correct explicit decision while proving the structural path no longer permits unsupported promotion.

## Outcome Capsule

- **Outcome**: For any behavior-changing OpenCode workflow, the agent keeps the scope of each completion, compatibility, safety, substitution, population-wide, or milestone claim within a declared evidence population and observation boundary. Representative proof remains an early working signal; broader completion requires explicit closure or an honestly narrowed, blocked, or unknown claim.
- **Operating Envelope**: Universal project and OpenSpec workflows loaded from the kit; Ordinary Small exact-case work remains concise; claim-evidence closure becomes mandatory when a result generalizes beyond its exercised cases, composes evidence from different paths, suppresses or substitutes existing behavior, depends on a modeled real system, or claims phase/milestone, compatibility, completeness, interchangeability, or safety scope.
- **Non-Goals**: Project- or domain-specific semantics; a consumer-specific rule, dataset, agent, or proof runner; a general theorem prover; exhaustive testing of infinite input spaces; automatic semantic partitioning or risk scoring; replacing project-native tests, Runtime Proof, critical SDET, safety assessment, or owner authority; making every Ordinary Small task enter full qualification.
- **Non-Deferrable Invariants**: Evidence never authorizes a broader claim than its observed identity, population, production path, environment, and oracle; offline or representative evidence cannot silently satisfy a reachable higher-fidelity claim; finite known populations close exhaustively or through an explicit reviewed exclusion/partition contract; helpers validate reviewed facts without inferring semantics; unknown or unobservable material dependencies narrow or block the claim; lifecycle labels never widen claim scope; reviewers and arbiters never authorize mutation or protected effects.
- **Observable Proof**: Through the actual loaded OpenCode entry point, run matched disposable scenarios showing that complete offline results for a finite population plus one representative real-system case support only the exact exercised case and leave the population claim blocked or unknown, a complete exact population can support only its declared environment claim, an unavailable real oracle keeps the dependent claim blocked or unknown, and an Ordinary Small exact-case task still completes through its existing proportional route. Deterministic contracts reject stale, incomplete, mismatched-path, or over-broad closure records.
- **Material Residual Risks**: Model-authored claim and partition semantics remain judgment rather than mathematical proof; a reviewed finite corpus cannot establish arbitrary unseen inputs; synthetic workflow evaluations cannot predict every model/project combination; independent review adds bounded latency to triggered broad claims; already active completion-runtime work must finish or transfer ownership before overlapping semantics change.
- **Stop Line**: Finish one universal claim-evidence capability, concise always-loaded routing, one on-demand substitution qualification skill, one read-only evidence-sufficiency reviewer, proportional OpenSpec capsule/archive integration, completion-arbiter closure handling, deterministic structural validation, and matched loaded-session behavioral proof. Do not add domain adapters, universal test generation, probabilistic scoring, release/deployment policy, or target-project changes.

## Claim And Evidence Scope

- **Claim ID**: `CEC-STRUCTURE`, `CEC-SUBSTITUTION`, `CEC-UNIVERSAL`, and `CEC-EXACT`; `claim-evidence-scope.md` owns the complete reviewed matrix.
- **Claim Class**: Phase/milestone, real-system equivalence, compatibility/interchangeability, and exact case.
- **Population**: The explicit structural fixtures, maintained portable surfaces, one synthetic substitution case, and one Ordinary Small exact case listed in the bounded evidence index.
- **Coverage Basis**: Reviewed finite or partitioned provider-free cases plus separately captured loaded exact scenarios; no unseen-input generalization.
- **Production Path**: Current OpenSpec operation owners, loaded kit routing, and installed completion boundary named by each claim record.
- **Comparison Paths**: Baseline and candidate paths are explicit only for substitution and matched loaded-session evidence; otherwise `null`.
- **Environment**: The recorded candidate/environment identities in `evidence-index.json`; no other project, machine, deployment, or external system.
- **Real Oracle**: Required only where the declared claim depends on the installed or owning real boundary; unavailable required observation blocks that claim.
- **Unresolved Observations**: Completion-runtime and loaded matched proof remain pending; current claim records retain `blocked` ceilings rather than infer success.
- **Maximum Claim**: Provider-free contract behavior and later captured installed scenarios only, bounded by each machine-readable `maximumSupportedClaim`.

## What Changes

- Add one universal claim-evidence closure model covering claim surface, evidence population, production and baseline paths, coverage basis, real oracle, unresolved observations, and maximum supported claim.
- Separate representative happy-path proof from accepted-scope completion: a representative case establishes only its exercised lane unless a reviewed coverage argument closes the broader population.
- Require finite known populations to use complete inventory closure or an explicit reviewed partition/exclusion contract; helper code validates identities, cardinality, statuses, and references without inventing semantic equivalence.
- Require modeled substitutions such as skip, omit, suppress, cache, replay, emulation, replacement, or optimized bypass to preserve the applicable baseline state, effects, order, timing, faults, recovery, cleanup, and terminal observations at the owning real boundary before a broader equivalence claim.
- Add an on-demand `behavioral-substitution-qualification` skill and a fresh read-only `evidence-sufficiency-reviewer` for triggered broad-claim closure. The reviewer challenges whether evidence entails the original outcome and maximum claim; main still owns every disposition and lifecycle decision.
- Extend OpenSpec proposal/apply/archive controls and the completion arbiter so missing, stale, mismatched-path, representative-only, or materially unknown closure cannot produce a broader complete/archive/stop claim.
- Add universal synthetic behavioral evaluations that reproduce the failure shape without embedding any consumer project, hardware, corpus, protocol, or product name in portable instructions.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `library-claim-evidence-closure`: Defines universal claim scope, evidence populations, coverage bases, real-oracle and substitution equivalence, independent challenge, honest narrowing/blocking, and deterministic closure facts.

### Modified Capabilities

- `library-change-ready-sdlc`: Separates representative Runtime Proof from broad completion evidence and routes triggered substitution or population-wide claims through claim-evidence closure.
- `library-instruction-artifacts`: Adds universal skill/agent routing, least-privilege review behavior, compact canonical ownership, and loaded-session anti-overclaim evaluations.
- `library-spec-workflow-integrity`: Extends the Spec Capsule and complete-archive checks with proportional claim-evidence closure while keeping Ordinary Small work concise.
- `library-consumer-outcome-regression`: Allows bounded focused decision-gap scenario packs to reuse matched loaded-session capture without changing the maintained general baseline or claiming universal improvement.
- `session-completion-guard`: Requires completion adjudication to preserve claim ceilings and continue when a current broad claim lacks matching population, path, or real-boundary evidence.
