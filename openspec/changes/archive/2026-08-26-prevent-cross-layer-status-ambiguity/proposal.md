## Why

User-facing and continuation status text can collapse several independent facts into one ambiguous phrase, causing a path-scoped `unknown` or `blocked` state to be read later as resource unavailability, missing authority, or a blocked accepted outcome. The canonical specification already requires plain and accurate communication, but the loaded instruction and compaction contracts do not explicitly preserve the subject and evidence scope of status words.

## Outcome Capsule

- **Outcome**: Global OpenCode instructions and continuation summaries attach status words to the exact subject and evidence scope, preserve independently known adjacent facts, and keep resource availability, authority, path/runner readiness, evidence completeness, operational consequence, and accepted-outcome state distinct.
- **Operating Envelope**: Kit-owned global instruction sources, maintained compaction prompt mirrors, the Change-Ready live-attempt output contract, deterministic source validation, and a finite synthetic status-scope behavior pack on one recorded configured model route; no consumer-project, hardware, external-service, or production operation is required.
- **Non-Goals**: Enforcing one prose template for every response, scoring writing quality, proving universal model compliance, changing live-attempt safety or authorization semantics, modifying PMAC-specific artifacts, or adding a wording reviewer, agent, or skill.
- **Non-Deferrable Invariants**: Brevity must not erase known facts; an `unknown` or `blocked` value in one dimension must not be promoted to another; missing evidence must not be inferred as clear; live-attempt and protected-boundary fail-closed behavior remains unchanged; compaction must not broaden or invert preserved state.
- **Observable Proof**: The installed loaded candidate and its compaction path correctly separate every expected status dimension in the maintained finite scenarios, preserve the known-resource positive case and unknown-resource negative control, state the path-scoped operational consequence, and pass deterministic source/mirror validation without unauthorized effects.
- **Material Residual Risks**: Natural-language compliance remains model- and context-sensitive outside the maintained population; explicit status separation can add small response length; future compaction or instruction consolidation can regress behavior unless the retained source marker and behavior pack remain current.
- **Stop Line**: Finish the smallest canonical wording change, exact Live-Attempt Gate delta, compaction mirror update, finite regression pack, installed loaded-source proof, and applicable project validation. Do not add a general prose linter, new practice owner, broad style framework, or project-specific policy.

## Claim And Evidence Scope

- **Claim ID**: CSA-001
- **Claim Class**: finite-population
- **Population**: The reviewed status-scope main-response and compaction/reconstruction scenarios, including known-resource/path-unknown and resource-unknown negative-control cases, on the selected configured route.
- **Coverage Basis**: Every reviewed scenario captured once per frozen baseline and readable candidate arm with identical prompt bytes, fixture facts, permissions, model, variant, OpenCode version, operating-system class, and evaluator semantics.
- **Production Path**: Installed OpenCode loading the candidate kit global instruction source and maintained compaction prompt through the verified active configuration.
- **Comparison Paths**: Frozen pre-change loaded source under the same runner and environment; provider-free replay of both preserved arms through the terminal evaluator.
- **Environment**: Recorded Windows class, installed OpenCode version, selected configured model/variant, isolated synthetic fixture, and effect-denied permission envelope.
- **Real Oracle**: Exact expected classification of resource availability, authority, proof-path readiness, evidence state, operational consequence, and accepted outcome, plus retained user-facing text and compaction reconstruction showing no cross-dimension inference.
- **Unresolved Observations**: Other models, variants, languages, unreviewed status vocabularies, long-session contexts, and consumer overrides remain unobserved.
- **Maximum Claim**: The recorded candidate preserved scoped status meaning for every maintained scenario under the recorded source, model, prompt, fixture, and environment; it does not prove universal prose quality or model adherence.

## Automation Dividend

- **Automation Dividend**: required - extend the existing consumer-outcome regression harness with one finite status-scope decision pack, stable expected fields, provider-free replay, and retained privacy-safe response text instead of creating a new runner or fuzzy prose scorer.

## What Changes

- Strengthen the canonical Principle of Least Surprise so status words identify their exact subject and evidence scope and do not imply state in an adjacent dimension.
- Require user-facing status reports and continuation/compaction summaries to keep resource, authority, path/runner, evidence, operational consequence, and accepted-outcome facts separate when their states differ.
- Tighten `Live-Attempt Gate` reporting so its classification applies only to the named invocation or proof path and its operational consequence is stated separately from resource availability, authority, environment, and overall outcome.
- Update maintained global and compaction prompt mirrors through their existing source/materialization ownership rather than adding project-local wording.
- Add deterministic source/mirror drift checks and the bounded installed behavior pack described by CSA-001.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `library-instruction-artifacts`: Require subject-scoped status wording and lossless status separation in user-facing and compaction communication, with bounded loaded-behavior evidence.
- `library-change-ready-sdlc`: Clarify that a Live-Attempt Gate classification and operational consequence are path-scoped and must not imply resource, authority, environment, or accepted-outcome state.

## Impact

- Canonical working philosophy, always-loaded global authority, Change-Ready output guidance, compaction prompt source and maintained mirrors, instruction validators/contracts, existing consumer-outcome proof harness and fixtures, proof inventory, and the two modified OpenSpec capabilities.
- No application API, persisted data, dependency, remote system, hardware behavior, or live-attempt authorization changes.
- Running OpenCode sessions will require a new session or supported reload after implementation before the updated instructions are active.
