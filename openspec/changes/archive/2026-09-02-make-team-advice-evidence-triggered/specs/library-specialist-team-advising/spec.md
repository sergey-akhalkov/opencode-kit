## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Non-trivial root tasks receive proportional team advice

After bounded foraging establishes the original goal, observed repository/task state, material unknowns, current candidate or worktree identity, and operating constraints, main SHALL execute a root mission without `specialist-team-advisor` only when every current direct-route fact is established: one known semantic owner, one already selected execution route, one representative proof boundary, no unresolved maintained-route choice, no unavailable required capability, no unique independent-evidence need, and no unresolved delegation or isolation boundary. An unsupported or stale fact SHALL NOT satisfy the predicate. Task size, file count, reversibility, or main's assertion that advice is unnecessary SHALL NOT independently establish direct eligibility.

Main SHALL obtain one fresh bounded recommendation when a concrete team-selection uncertainty remains after foraging: two or more plausible maintained routes with materially different value or boundaries, an exact required capability whose availability or owner is unresolved, a unique independent-evidence package whose selection can change the next accepted action, or a useful isolation/delegation package whose boundary is unresolved. A root mission episode begins when a parentless user-facing root session accepts a new outcome that is not a continuation of its current accepted mission. Child specialists, guard/compaction sessions, campaign executors, and propose/apply commands continuing the same outcome SHALL NOT begin another episode.

Registered Practice Owner triggers remain independent and SHALL NOT require advisor discovery. `direct` SHALL mean omission of team advice only. It SHALL NOT bypass a matched Practice Owner, Change-Ready Material route, LFTD, OPDC, CCO, OpenSpec operation gate, substitution qualification, runtime proof, validation, writer-liveness, authorization, safety, or protected-action boundary. Once advice is actually obtained, the advisor MAY return `main-alone` when no available package adds unique current value.

#### Scenario: Complete direct predicate stays direct

- **WHEN** current evidence establishes one semantic owner, one selected route, one representative proof boundary, no maintained-route choice, no unavailable required capability, no unique independent-evidence need, and no unresolved delegation or isolation boundary
- **THEN** main proceeds without team advice even when the work is non-trivial
- **AND** every independently matched practice, dependency, proof, safety, and authorization contract remains active.

#### Scenario: Trivial owner-local work stays direct

- **WHEN** current evidence shows one action in one known owner with a selected route and proof boundary and no unresolved team-selection fact
- **THEN** main proceeds without team advice
- **AND** the ordinary outcome, safety, worktree, proof, and validation floor remains unchanged.

#### Scenario: One direct fact is unknown

- **WHEN** a package-dependent next action lacks current evidence for its owner, route, required capability, independent-evidence need, or isolation boundary
- **THEN** main does not declare the complete direct predicate satisfied
- **AND** obtains one advisor map when the active catalog can resolve that concrete team-selection uncertainty.

#### Scenario: New non-trivial root task receives one advisory pass

- **WHEN** two plausible maintained routes could change the next accepted action and current evidence does not select between their unique values or boundaries
- **THEN** main obtains one fresh bounded recommendation before package-dependent execution
- **AND** retains final route selection, integration, proof, and disposition.

#### Scenario: Campaign executor is a child control plane

- **WHEN** a campaign controller creates a child executor for work already decomposed under another mission control plane
- **THEN** the child does not treat startup as a new user-root advisory episode
- **AND** any future campaign integration remains separately specified rather than inferred here.

#### Scenario: Exact Practice Owner activates without advice

- **WHEN** one registered Practice Owner trigger is exact and no team-selection uncertainty exists
- **THEN** main invokes that owner directly without using the advisor as discovery
- **AND** the owner remains read-only and non-authorizing under its exact practice contract.

#### Scenario: Direct route still loads another required contract

- **WHEN** the direct predicate is complete but the accepted work independently matches Change-Ready, LFTD, OPDC, CCO, substitution, OpenSpec, or a safety gate
- **THEN** main omits only `specialist-team-advisor`
- **AND** follows the independently matched contract before its dependent action.

#### Scenario: Same mission moves from propose to apply

- **WHEN** a parentless root session continues one accepted mission from OpenSpec proposal into apply without a material team-topology change
- **THEN** the command transition does not begin a second advisory episode
- **AND** any existing engagement state remains subject only to its recorded reconsultation condition.

#### Scenario: Advisor recommends main-alone

- **WHEN** concrete uncertainty activated the advisor but the current catalog shows no package adds unique evidence or bounded execution value
- **THEN** it returns `main-alone` with exact evidence and a reconsultation condition
- **AND** main dispatches no ceremonial specialist.

#### Scenario: Catalog failure is scoped to the required choice

- **WHEN** the catalog is unavailable and one accepted item depends on a catalog-backed package choice while another item satisfies the complete direct predicate
- **THEN** only the package-dependent item remains unknown or blocked
- **AND** main continues the independent direct item without mission-wide Team Advice state.

#### Scenario: Existing advice remains current

- **WHEN** a mission already has an advisor map and no outcome, evidence, candidate, catalog, integration, ownership, or activation topology changed materially
- **THEN** main does not call the advisor again
- **AND** preserves only current packages, evidence references, activation facts, and the reconsultation condition.
