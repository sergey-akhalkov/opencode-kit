## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Outcome accountability and practice responsibility stay separate

Main SHALL retain the accepted mission outcome, scope, final implementation and integration decisions, Runtime Proof, finding disposition, lifecycle state, and owner handoff in Ordinary Small and Material work. Main's primary function SHALL be mission success through goal control, decomposition, resource selection, dependency management, evidence-based course correction, integration, and proof rather than maximizing direct execution or specialist use. Main SHALL omit team advice only when the complete current direct-route predicate is established. When a concrete team-selection uncertainty remains, main SHALL use one bounded team-advice pass while retaining the mission spine and every integration decision. A registered Practice Owner SHALL own only its practice semantics, trigger applicability, runtime observation, and maintenance consistency. Team advisors, production workers, SDET, optional reviewers, and control-plane agents SHALL consume applicable practice constraints without becoming outcome owners or additional orchestrators.

Direct work SHALL be an orchestration choice only. It SHALL NOT change the task's Ordinary Small or Material profile, suppress an exact Practice Owner, authorize a protected effect, satisfy LFTD/OPDC/CCO/OpenSpec/substitution requirements, replace runtime proof or validation, or weaken dependency, writer-liveness, worktree, cleanup, safety, privacy, authorization, or data-integrity invariants. When a practice controls a concrete implementation decision, main SHALL make and integrate the decision against the accepted outcome after considering owner evidence. The owner SHALL evaluate the practice but SHALL NOT select or authorize the product decision. Advice MAY identify the boundary but SHALL NOT decide it.

#### Scenario: Complete direct predicate leaves Material routing intact

- **WHEN** one owner, route, and proof boundary are known with no team-selection uncertainty, but the work changes loaded lifecycle or safety policy
- **THEN** main omits team advice and still loads the Material Change-Ready route before mutation
- **AND** retains every required planning, proof, validation, and safety gate.

#### Scenario: Change locality is materially triggered

- **WHEN** accepted variation, mixed responsibility, state transition, system boundary, or important invariant makes change locality material
- **THEN** the exact `architecture-and-change-locality` owner reviews the named practice boundary without advisor discovery
- **AND** main selects and proves the concrete direct implementation or smallest useful seam.

#### Scenario: Practice Owner recommends broader scope

- **WHEN** an owner report proposes work outside the accepted outcome or enforced envelope
- **THEN** main treats the proposal as non-authorizing evidence and keeps current scope unchanged
- **AND** only an independently required dependency closure or explicit owner scope decision can expand it.

#### Scenario: Worker receives an owned-practice constraint

- **WHEN** main delegates a production slice after a Practice Owner identified an applicable invariant
- **THEN** the brief carries the exact invariant and bounded evidence reference
- **AND** the worker preserves it or reports a conflict without invoking or impersonating the owner.

#### Scenario: Main retains the mission spine

- **WHEN** team advice identifies bounded research, implementation, or review packages
- **THEN** main retains outcome interpretation, dependencies, cross-package ownership, integration, representative Runtime Proof inspection, course correction, and final disposition
- **AND** delegates only packages whose focused context or isolation has evidenced value greater than handoff and integration cost.

#### Scenario: Direct work is a tactic rather than main's objective

- **WHEN** main can execute a bounded package directly without team advice
- **THEN** it still follows every independently triggered owner, lifecycle, proof, validation, dependency, and safety contract
- **AND** direct status supplies no authorization or semantic exemption.

#### Scenario: Advisor prepares a production delegation boundary

- **WHEN** a non-trivial task contains one isolated production package with exact ownership, acceptance, proof input, and forbidden actions but delegation value or boundary remains unresolved
- **THEN** the engagement map may return a dispatch-ready implementation boundary
- **AND** main verifies it and remains responsible for dispatch liveness, integration, and current proof.

#### Scenario: Specialist evidence is verified without wholesale rework

- **WHEN** a specialist returns a terminal attributable result with assumptions, artifacts, evidence, and validation
- **THEN** main verifies critical facts, integration, candidate identity, and observable behavior at the sufficient boundary
- **AND** repeats deep work only when contradiction, stale evidence, unexplained failure, or material risk makes the result insufficient.

### Requirement: Practice routing preserves proportional delivery

Team advice and practice ownership SHALL not create an all-artifact checklist, mandatory architecture phase, or fixed reviewer sequence. Main SHALL obtain no advice when every direct-route fact is current, invoke no owner for a zero-trigger concern, invoke an exact Practice Owner directly when triggered, and use at most one fresh bounded recommendation for a current concrete team-selection uncertainty. Catalog unavailability SHALL block only a required catalog-dependent choice. Multiple independent reports SHALL be batched only when the discovered runtime supports it and scopes are isolated or exact non-overlapping; otherwise they SHALL be serialized without repeating the same concern.

Advisor and specialist reports SHALL be bounded to decision-relevant evidence and SHALL not restate the complete global philosophy, task transcript, active catalog, or another role's rules. Main SHALL integrate the result rather than paste it into later briefs or handoffs unless an exact finding, work boundary, or evidence reference remains material.

#### Scenario: One advisor recommendation is sufficient

- **WHEN** one engagement map resolves current team topology and no material topology change occurs
- **THEN** main proceeds without repeated advisor calls
- **AND** retains only current packages, activation conditions, evidence references, and invalidation facts.

#### Scenario: Complete direct predicate exists

- **WHEN** every direct-route fact is current and no catalog-backed package decision can change the next accepted action
- **THEN** main does not call the advisor or preserve empty Team Advice state
- **AND** continues through the nearest representative proof boundary.

#### Scenario: Direct predicate is incomplete for one item

- **WHEN** one accepted item has a concrete team-selection uncertainty and an independent sibling has a complete direct route
- **THEN** main consults only for the dependent item
- **AND** continues the independent direct sibling without broadening the blocker.

#### Scenario: One owner is sufficient

- **WHEN** one exact owner report resolves the only material practice uncertainty for the next decision
- **THEN** main proceeds without optional owner or reviewer fan-out
- **AND** retains only decision-relevant evidence.

#### Scenario: Owner reports overlap

- **WHEN** two owner reports address the same concern despite registered boundary separation
- **THEN** main uses the registry to identify the primary practice and preserves both evidence sources
- **AND** does not ask the team advisor to adjudicate practice semantics.

#### Scenario: Fixed lifecycle team is rejected

- **WHEN** an engagement map recommends roles only because planning, implementation, testing, review, or completion stages exist
- **THEN** main rejects those recommendations unless each role has a unique current question, activation evidence, and expected output
- **AND** preserves the direct or smaller team route.

#### Scenario: Catalog outage is scoped

- **WHEN** the advisor catalog is unavailable and an independent accepted item does not depend on that catalog decision
- **THEN** only the capability-dependent decision remains unknown
- **AND** main continues every complete direct item without a synthetic mission-wide blocker.
