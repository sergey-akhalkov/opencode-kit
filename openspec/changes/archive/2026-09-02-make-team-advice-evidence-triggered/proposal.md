## Why

The kit currently obtains team advice for nearly every new non-trivial root mission even when current evidence already identifies one owner, one selected route, and one representative proof boundary. The existing objective trivial bypass is safe but too narrow, while the earlier combined `streamline-ordinary-small-closure` plan made the opposite mistake by letting main decide circularly that no advisor was useful. This change defines an objective evidence-triggered boundary for direct execution without weakening exact Practice Owner, Material, proof, or safety routes.

## Outcome Capsule

- **Outcome**: Every installed kit primary session executes a root mission without `specialist-team-advisor` only when current evidence satisfies one objective direct-route predicate: one known semantic owner, one already selected execution route, one representative proof boundary, no maintained-route choice, no unavailable required capability, no unique independent-evidence need, and no unresolved delegation or isolation boundary. Every concrete team-selection uncertainty still receives one bounded advisor map, and `direct` means advisor omission only rather than exemption from another lifecycle or practice contract.
- **Operating Envelope**: Loaded global main and maintained project instructions, the existing unrestricted local agent-permission policy, the role-bounded `specialist-team-advisor`, the technically fail-closed privacy-safe catalog boundary, current Practice Owner routing, STA-001 consumer-outcome scenarios, ordinary and OpenSpec parentless root missions, and bounded configured sessions in disposable repositories; OPDC, LFTD, CCO, and the narrowed Ordinary Small artifact-contract change are archived, and production mutation uses only the exact transferred owners after current conflict-free readback.
- **Non-Goals**: Removing the advisor or catalog, changing Practice Owner triggers, making main skip `change-ready-sdlc`, leaf-first decomposition, delivery checkpoints, capability-boundary decisions, runtime proof, substitution qualification, safety or protected-action gates, adding a deterministic semantic router, changing OpenSpec artifact-profile semantics, adding another team planner, or optimizing all specialist latency.
- **Non-Deferrable Invariants**: Main retains mission scope, integration, proof, finding disposition, and protected decisions. Direct eligibility requires every predicate fact to be current; unknown or competing package facts do not silently become direct. Advisor omission never suppresses exact Practice Owners or other matched contracts. Catalog failure blocks only a catalog-dependent choice. Advice remains non-authorizing. Child and writer liveness, worktree preservation, privacy, safety, authorization, cleanup, and current evidence ceilings remain unchanged.
- **Observable Proof**: Through the actual installed OpenCode entry point, run matched disposable scenarios for objective direct work, a maintained-route choice, an unavailable required capability, a unique fresh-evidence need, an unresolved isolation boundary, an exact Practice Owner trigger, catalog failure with independent direct work, and continuity of an already advised mission. Observe zero advisor/catalog calls only for the complete direct predicate, exactly one advisor for each concrete selection uncertainty, direct Practice Owner activation, scoped catalog failure, unchanged representative runtime proof and safety/effect oracles, and terminal process/session/root cleanup.
- **Material Residual Risks**: The direct predicate remains model-applied and may over-call or under-call the advisor; stale owner or proof facts can produce false direct eligibility; the unrestricted local runtime technically exposes tools that advisor role text forbids; catalog caller/root attribution constrains catalog disclosure but does not enforce every role prohibition; always-loaded wording can conflict with LFTD, OPDC, CCO, or Material routing; finite STA-001 scenarios do not prove universal classification; overlapping loaded-main, mirror, validator, and proof roots require pairwise ownership dependencies.
- **Stop Line**: Finish one objective direct predicate, explicit `direct` semantics, evidence-triggered advisor activation, scoped catalog failure, exact Practice Owner independence, mission continuity, focused structural contracts, and matched installed behavior proof. Stop before OpenSpec artifact-profile changes, another planner/registry, deterministic semantic inference, broader specialist lifecycle redesign, consumer-project implementation, installation, activation, publication, or remote effects.
- **Delivery Horizon:** none - this is a universal kit routing correction and is not part of a declared product delivery horizon.

## What Changes

- Replace the universal non-trivial-root advisor trigger with an objective direct-route predicate and concrete team-selection uncertainty triggers.
- Define `direct` as omission of team advice only; preserve every independently matched practice, lifecycle, proof, safety, and authorization route.
- Preserve exact Practice Owner activation without advisor discovery and keep catalog failure scoped to the package-dependent choice.
- Rebind the maintained STA-001 single-domain control from one `main-alone` advisor call to zero advisor/catalog calls while retaining positive advisor members.
- Keep Team Advice State absent until advice actually exists and retain current reconsultation semantics after advice.
- Use stable routing identifiers and installed behavior fixtures instead of complete-sentence mirrors for the touched Team Advice route.
- Begin production mutation only while every predecessor remains archived, exact pairwise ownership transfers are current, and the ownership inventory stays conflict-free.
