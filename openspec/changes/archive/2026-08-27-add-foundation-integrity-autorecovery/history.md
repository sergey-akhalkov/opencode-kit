## 2026-08-26 - Extend Outcome Readiness Or Claim Evidence

- **Objective:** Close the wrong-foundation recurrence without adding another core Practice Owner.
- **Approach:** Put workload/profile/environment/oracle comparison into either `implementation-readiness-reviewer` or `evidence-sufficiency-reviewer` and reuse its current launch trigger.
- **Evidence:** Current contracts route outcome readiness when the outcome/envelope/first boundary is unresolved and claim evidence only for declared broad claims. The recurrence can arise after readiness and before a broad claim, while locally green implementation and proof continue accumulating. The instruction-governance and architecture planning reviews independently identified the cross-identity foundation relation as a distinct responsibility.
- **Outcome:** Rejected for this change.
- **Reason:** Either extension would miss part of the reachable event window and give one owner two independent change axes; it would also make broad-claim declaration a prerequisite for early foundation detection.
- **Do Not Repeat Until:** Current owner contracts or matched runtime evidence show the foundation relation is always and uniquely reached by one existing trigger without widening Ordinary Small review.
- **Evidence-Based Retry Condition:** A maintained scenario proves the distinct owner adds no decision-changing observation while one existing owner reliably detects the same bind/rebind contradiction earlier with lower total routing cost and no overlap.

## 2026-08-26 - Add A Semantic Plugin, Score, Or Foundation Database

- **Objective:** Make detection deterministic and persistent across sessions and projects.
- **Approach:** Add a provider plugin or helper that extracts identities from repository text, scores mismatch likelihood, stores project foundation records, and launches recovery above a threshold.
- **Evidence:** Workload, profile, configuration, environment, and oracle meaning is project-domain semantics rather than a stable textual shape. Existing deterministic-automation rules forbid model-like inference, ranking, or scoring in helpers, and project memory is advisory rather than normative authority. The accepted increment needs one current relation and bounded local recovery, not a new storage lifecycle.
- **Outcome:** Rejected.
- **Reason:** It would turn heuristic output into product authority, add migration/privacy/staleness responsibilities, and still require semantic model review and main disposition.
- **Do Not Repeat Until:** A reviewed cross-domain schema and authority model exists independently of this change and deterministic extraction has direct source-backed correctness evidence.
- **Evidence-Based Retry Condition:** At least three maintained independent consumers expose the same explicit machine-readable relation contract, and a deterministic parser can validate it without text inference, scoring, project-memory authority, or new protected decisions.

## 2026-08-26 - Combine Detection And Recovery In One Agent

- **Objective:** Minimize handoffs by letting one specialist detect, plan, edit, sweep, and approve the corrected foundation.
- **Approach:** Create a mutation-capable foundation agent with repository-wide planning authority and make its terminal report the recovery decision.
- **Evidence:** Practice Owners are read-only and non-authorizing; main is the sole outcome, mutation, integration, proof, and owner-question authority. A combined agent would use its own finding to authorize its writes, introduce another orchestrator and concurrent-writer lifecycle, and make one context both author and reviewer.
- **Outcome:** Rejected.
- **Reason:** The apparent handoff saving weakens evidence-not-authority, writer safety, and independent corrected-candidate review.
- **Do Not Repeat Until:** The user explicitly transfers outcome authority and the runtime supplies isolated writer ownership plus an independent post-write review boundary.
- **Evidence-Based Retry Condition:** Repeated matched evidence shows main-owned recovery is the causal bottleneck and a bounded mutation agent can reduce it without self-authorization, overlapping writes, lost context, or Ordinary Small overhead.
