## Why

The kit can produce locally correct implementation, runtime, reviewer, and claim-
ceiling evidence while later increments compose those results on a globally wrong
foundation. No current Practice Owner compares the accepted product outcome with
the current workload/population, profile/configuration, environment, and real-oracle
identities at the moment they are bound or rebound, and a one-time project risk
audit does not automatically invalidate dependent active work when that foundation
later proves wrong.

## What Changes

- Add one core `foundation-integrity` practice with a fresh read-only
  `foundation-integrity-reviewer`. It detects only evidence-backed, current,
  materially reachable contradictions among accepted outcome, representative
  workload/population, profile/configuration, environment, and observation-oracle
  identities. It remains quiet for Ordinary Small exact-case work, aligned bindings,
  historical evidence already held below its honest ceiling, architecture polish,
  and speculative future risk.
- Add one on-demand `foundation-integrity-recovery` procedure executed by main after
  main independently reproduces a reviewer finding. It falsifies before mutation,
  creates or updates the smallest corrective project-native planning artifacts,
  preserves and narrows prior evidence, serially sweeps every materially dependent
  active change and current specification, and resumes the accepted outcome on the
  corrected foundation without waiting for plan/spec/task approval.
- Route one compact bind/rebind and named-contradiction trigger through the existing
  Practice Ownership architecture. Do not add a second orchestrator, universal
  every-task review, numeric/fuzzy risk scoring, a semantic plugin, or project-memory
  authority.
- Require recovery to admit only a confirmed, current, reachable foundation defect
  that can affect the accepted outcome or a non-deferrable invariant. Non-critical,
  stylistic, optional, future-scope, or architecture-improvement findings MUST NOT
  create a change, task, backlog, review loop, or completion gate.
- Give each confirmed mismatch one stable incident identity and one bounded recovery
  episode. The same finding on the same candidate cannot open another change; main
  performs at most one fresh foundation re-review per corrected candidate, and a
  closed incident cannot reopen without new decision-changing identity or evidence.
- Preserve archived artifacts byte-for-byte. Current claims and active changes may
  reclassify archived results to a narrower historical, cross-profile, component, or
  regression ceiling, but recovery never rewrites or deletes history.
- Add deterministic structural validation and matched loaded-session behavior proof
  for a generalized wrong-workload/wrong-oracle recurrence, aligned and Ordinary
  Small negative controls, false-positive falsification, dependent-versus-unrelated
  active-change sweep, protected product ambiguity, archive non-rewrite, and
  unchanged-hypothesis anti-loop behavior.

## Outcome Capsule

- **Outcome:** Default core sessions detect a current material foundation-identity
  contradiction before further dependent investment, independently reproduce it,
  autonomously correct and sweep only affected planning/claim surfaces, and continue
  the original accepted outcome without an unnecessary owner hop or polishing loop.
- **Operating Envelope:** Portable OpenCode-kit instruction/runtime behavior; current
  accepted outcomes and named workload/population, profile/configuration,
  environment, and oracle identities; fresh read-only Practice Owner evidence;
  main-owned local project planning/spec/claim mutation; serial active-change sweep;
  disposable local behavior fixtures; no protected or external action.
- **Non-Goals:** Guaranteeing discovery of every project risk; generic architecture
  review; code-quality cleanup; automatic product-semantic selection; every-task or
  scheduled review; numeric probability; heuristic helper classification; provider
  plugin; persistent global risk register; normative project-memory storage; product
  implementation in consumer repositories; archive rewrite; deployment, release,
  remote mutation, credentials, cost, or protected action.
- **Non-Deferrable Invariants:** Main remains sole Outcome Owner and mutation owner;
  reviewer output is evidence rather than authority; reproduction/falsification
  precedes semantic mutation; recovery requires a confirmed reachable current
  foundation defect; non-critical findings create no work; unrelated changes remain
  untouched; prior evidence survives at its truthful narrower ceiling; one incident
  cannot recursively mint successor incidents from the same finding/candidate;
  owner contact remains limited to a genuinely unresolved protected product decision
  after safe discriminating work is exhausted.
- **Observable Proof:** Same-model disposable baseline/candidate workflows show the
  candidate alone launches the exact owner for a generalized cross-family workload/
  oracle mismatch, main falsifies or confirms before mutation, a confirmed unique
  correction updates only dependent active artifacts and narrows old evidence while
  preserving archives, one corrected-candidate re-review terminates the episode, and
  aligned, historical-only, Ordinary Small, non-critical architecture, and repeated-
  unchanged-finding controls create no recovery work. Structural validation proves
  exact registry/profile/catalog/permission/context-quality and skill/agent schema
  integrity.
- **Material Residual Risks:** Model judgment remains probabilistic; incomplete or
  stale project authority may leave applicability `unknown`; a consumer without a
  project-native planning surface can receive direct main correction but not an
  invented OpenSpec repository; adding another core owner must preserve context
  quality and loaded behavior without weakening current owners; inventory growth
  remains diagnostic; the first increment cannot prove detection of unrepresented
  project-domain mismatches.
- **Stop Line:** Finish one new core practice owner, one main-executed recovery skill,
  compact routing, active-change propagation and anti-loop contracts, profile/catalog/
  validator integration, and matched loaded behavior evidence. Do not add a plugin,
  risk database, automatic semantic scorer, project-memory extension, consumer-
  project migration, generic architect, or another completion/orchestration layer.

### Claim And Evidence Scope

- **Claim ID**: `foundation-integrity-autorecovery-v1`
- **Claim Class**: partitioned-domain workflow behavior
- **Population**: Reviewed disposable partitions for a current cross-family
  foundation mismatch, aligned binding, historical-only evidence, Ordinary Small
  exact case, non-critical architecture polish, false-positive reviewer finding,
  confirmed unique correction,
  dependent-versus-unrelated active changes, overlapping dependent ownership,
  protected product ambiguity, archive preservation, and unchanged-hypothesis
  anti-loop behavior under the selected core profile.
- **Coverage Basis**: Versioned generic fixtures, deterministic structural checks,
  same-model baseline/candidate configured OpenCode captures, direct filesystem
  readback of disposable project artifacts, exact child-role attribution, and explicit
  terminal rows for every reviewed partition.
- **Production Path**: Loaded primary main-session routing through the registered
  `foundation-integrity` Practice Owner, main-owned reproduction/disposition, and the
  on-demand recovery procedure against the current project-native planning surface.
- **Comparison Paths**: Unchanged kit baseline versus the candidate loaded runtime;
  detected mismatch versus aligned/no-trigger controls; confirmed recovery versus
  falsified and protected-ambiguity branches.
- **Environment**: Current local OpenCode installation/configuration with disposable
  local repositories and no consumer-product, remote, network-service, deployment,
  controller, machine, or destructive effect.
- **Real Oracle**: Actual configured root/child routing and artifact mutations in
  disposable repositories, inspected after terminal completion; deterministic
  validators are structural support and do not replace loaded behavior evidence.
- **Unresolved Observations**: Provider/model generalization beyond the exercised
  configured model; consumer projects with missing, contradictory, or unreadable
  current authority; non-OpenSpec planning systems beyond the generic main-owned
  correction contract; project-domain mismatch classes absent from the reviewed
  partition set.
- **Maximum Claim**: The exercised configured model and reviewed generic partitions
  detect, autonomously correct, sweep, and terminate the represented foundation-
  integrity episodes without regressing negative controls. This does not establish
  universal risk discovery, semantic correctness for every consumer project, or
  autonomous authority over unresolved protected product decisions.

- **Automation Dividend**: required - extend one existing disposable configured-
  session behavior harness with a foundation-integrity partition pack and use
  deterministic tooling only to validate reviewed registry, profile, artifact,
  incident-state, and filesystem identities without scoring or inferring semantics.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `library-foundation-integrity`: Define event-triggered foundation relation review,
  evidence-backed material-risk admission, main-owned autonomous recovery, dependent
  active-change propagation, truthful evidence narrowing, and anti-polishing/
  anti-loop termination.

### Modified Capabilities

- `library-practice-ownership`: Register one distinct core foundation-integrity
  Practice Owner with exact triggers, exclusions, authority, and maintenance mode.
- `library-change-ready-sdlc`: Require main to reproduce and autonomously recover a
  confirmed foundation defect while preserving protected owner boundaries and
  parking non-critical findings.
- `library-spec-workflow-integrity`: Define serial all-active-change impact sweep,
  corrective delta ownership, task reopening, claim narrowing, and archive
  preservation after foundation invalidation.
- `library-instruction-artifacts`: Define the new reviewer/skill/routing contracts,
  context-quality discipline, validation surfaces, and matched behavior fixtures.
- `library-runtime-surface-profiles`: Include the new core owner and recovery skill in
  selected runtime manifests with separate context diagnostics, context-quality
  checks, and loaded behavior gates.

## Impact

- **Instruction runtime:** one new global read-only subagent, one on-demand main-owned
  skill, and compact existing routing integration. Running OpenCode sessions will
  require restart after later implementation installs these config-time artifacts.
- **Practice ownership:** registry, generated ownership view, exact roster count,
  profile bindings, catalogs, validators, and focused proofs change coherently.
- **OpenSpec workflow:** confirmed foundation invalidation gains a portable serial
  active-change sweep and corrective-delta path; archives remain immutable and main
  specs continue to synchronize only through normal OpenSpec ownership/archive flow.
- **Behavior proof:** existing generic configured-session and Practice Owner routing
  harnesses gain bounded foundation-integrity partitions; no consumer project is
  mutated by proposal work or by repository validation.
- **Dependencies/public surfaces:** `replace-instruction-limits-with-context-quality`
  must apply first, or this change must explicitly rebase on its context-quality
  contract before mutation. No external dependency, public product API,
  persisted-data format, protocol, deployment, provider plugin, or remote interface is
  added. The instruction/runtime behavior is intentionally Material because it
  changes loaded lifecycle routing and autonomous planning recovery.
