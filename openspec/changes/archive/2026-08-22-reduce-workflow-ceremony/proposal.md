## Why

The kit's workflow authority now spends substantial context, model calls, planning text, and completion time on duplicated qualification policy and mandatory process evidence that does not improve the proven product result. The current source also contains a direct split-brain contract: normative specs require a final-history retrospective while the validator rejects that ceremony, so structurally green validation can coexist with contradictory runtime authority.

## Outcome Capsule

- **Outcome**: OpenCode work keeps real-boundary proof, protected-action controls, failure visibility, independent critical-risk challenge, and an always-active philosophy of quality, shortest verified path, autonomy, token economy, continuous improvement, and authorized impediment removal while eliminating mandatory retrospective work, repeated per-task boilerplate, duplicated always-loaded Material policy, unconditional lifecycle labels for ordinary changes, avoidable completion-arbiter calls, misleading context budgets, false self-doctor blockers, and completion-guard permission overrides.
- **Operating Envelope**: The kit's global and project instruction artifacts, OpenSpec propose/apply/archive workflows, completion guard, configured roadmap mission integration, validators, doctor, inventory/budget tooling, templates, docs, and disposable local OpenCode qualification environments. No consumer repository is migrated automatically.
- **Non-Goals**: Weaker protected-boundary rules; replacing real-boundary proof with unit/static checks; deleting unique failed or terminal raw evidence; weakening live-attempt, writer-liveness, cleanup, or dirty-worktree safety; removing all reviewers or critical SDET; redesigning OpenSpec itself; changing public product APIs; or modifying unrelated active-change behavior without explicit ownership reconciliation.
- **Non-Deferrable Invariants**: Real happy-path execution and applicable validation remain required; protected, remote, destructive, credentialed, costly, and irreversible actions remain separately owner-controlled; explicit per-agent permissions remain effective; unknown writer/liveness and unqualified absence evidence remain fail-closed; current user and teammate work remains untouched; no green validator may accept contradictory normative and runtime workflow contracts.
- **Observable Proof**: Through a fresh disposable installed OpenCode source, demonstrate a bounded Ordinary Small change and a Material change with the same safety decisions as baseline but less startup context and no removed ceremony; demonstrate OpenSpec propose/apply/archive without a mandatory retrospective; demonstrate compact task deltas; demonstrate a certified deterministic grind completion with zero arbiter call and an ambiguous root that still invokes the arbiter; demonstrate project permission precedence; and run doctor against both the kit checkout and a consumer fixture with correct gate results.
- **Material Residual Risks**: Instruction reductions may change model adherence across providers; removing automatic SDET from non-critical Material work can reduce independent challenge if risk classification is wrong; deterministic completion must not accept a forged or stale certificate; existing active changes may retain historical task ceremony; and machine-local config continues to require explicit synchronization and a new OpenCode process.
- **Stop Line**: Finish when canonical specs and validators agree, global startup authority meets the reviewed compact target and enforces the working philosophy without weaker safety, new OpenSpec changes use one shared proof envelope plus task-specific deltas and no final retrospective, Ordinary Small handoffs omit RC bookkeeping, SDET is risk-triggered while focused regression ownership remains available, the guard preserves config permissions and uses the certified deterministic fast path, budget and doctor reports are truthful, installed disposable behavior is proven, and applicable validation is green. Do not rewrite archived evidence, bulk-delete current raw bundles, auto-migrate consumer repositories, or add another scoring, retrospective, workflow, or policy engine.

## What Changes

- **BREAKING** Remove the normative final-history retrospective and generated `Session-Derived Improvements` completion scope from OpenSpec authoring, apply, archive, compaction, specs, validators, and current compatible task controls. Optional workflow learning moves to explicit audit/feedback paths and never blocks product completion.
- Replace repeated per-task fidelity/safety/evidence questionnaires with one change-level proof envelope; each task records only behavior-specific deltas, dependencies, observable completion, and validation.
- Reduce always-loaded `global/AGENTS.md` to compact operational routing and safety invariants. Keep detailed Material qualification, evidence topology, and SDET procedure in `change-ready-sdlc`; keep role and project artifacts pointer- or delta-only.
- Put the durable working philosophy and priority order in explicitly loaded `global/principles-of-work.md`; open `global/AGENTS.md` with a canonical-owner pointer and enforce both surfaces deterministically.
- **BREAKING** Ordinary Small work reports working/blocked outcome, representative proof, validation, and limitations without RC numbering or stable promotion. RC/stable remains available only for explicit qualification, project-required qualification, or qualifying Material work.
- **BREAKING** Make fresh SDET mandatory only for a named reachable critical-risk class or explicit project/owner qualification requirement. Main may author focused regression tests after current happy-path proof; SDET remains independent, test-only, and critical-focused when invoked.
- Add a fail-closed deterministic completion certificate path for grind roots whose owning workflow can prove terminal requirements without inference. Ambiguous, stale, missing, owner-required, or unresolved roots continue through the hidden arbiter.
- **BREAKING** Stop the completion guard from replacing the merged top-level OpenCode permission policy. Normal config precedence controls main permissions and explicit specialist restrictions remain intact.
- Replace the grandfathered catalog/global budget view with separate startup-visible, discovery-metadata, and on-demand-body measurements; enforce a reviewed compact startup-authority target and prevent baseline regeneration from silently accepting growth.
- Make doctor recognize the kit's intentional `REPO_AGENTS.md` plus `global/` authority and repository-native validation while retaining consumer-project AGENTS/adapter requirements for actual consumers.
- Add cross-artifact consistency validation so normative specs, workflow prompts, validators, templates, and installed-source diagnostics cannot require and forbid the same ceremony simultaneously.
- Define evidence retention/indexing that preserves unique failed and terminal bundles while preventing broad evidence-tree ingestion from becoming a routine context requirement.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `library-instruction-artifacts`: Remove completion-retrospective policy, compact startup authority ownership, enforce the working philosophy and authorized impediment removal, separate context-cost classes, and require cross-artifact consistency and proportional task guidance.
- `library-spec-workflow-integrity`: Replace generated process-improvement completion scope with direct product completion and one shared change proof envelope plus per-task deltas.
- `library-change-ready-sdlc`: Make Ordinary Small lifecycle reporting proportional, make critical SDET risk-triggered, and allow main-owned focused regression tests after proof without weakening Material safety gates.
- `session-completion-guard`: Add fail-closed certified deterministic completion and preserve resolved permission policy instead of forcing top-level allow.
- `library-config-portability`: Preserve normal permission precedence, align source/template/installed compaction behavior, and expose truthful startup/discovery/on-demand diagnostics.
- `library-install-init-resilience`: Make doctor distinguish the kit checkout from a bootstrapped consumer while retaining explicit structural, qualification, and unattended gates.

- Runtime instructions and role contracts under `global/AGENTS.md`, `global/skills/`, `global/agents/`, `global/commands/`, project templates, and reusable instruction sources.
- OpenSpec authoring/apply/archive contracts, current normative specs, validators, and active changes that still contain the removed retrospective task.
- Completion guard controller/runtime support, mission executor integration, guard tests/proofs, and plugin/config permission validation.
- Instruction inventory/budget seed and validators, doctor/project classification, README/token-economy/quality guidance, and installed-source diagnostics.

- No new package dependency, public network API, remote operation, consumer migration, commit, push, release, or deployment is part of this change.
