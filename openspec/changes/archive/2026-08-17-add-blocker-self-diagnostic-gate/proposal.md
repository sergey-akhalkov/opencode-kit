## Why

The kit can preserve diagnostics, reject repeated strategies, and route a hard blocker to `troubleshooter`, yet an agent can still trust a misconfigured observer or stale runtime assumption and prematurely report a product or owner blocker. A bounded self-diagnostic gate is needed now so contradictory, zero, empty, or absence-based evidence triggers verification of the proof path before another costly attempt or user escalation.

## Outcome Capsule

- **Outcome**: Before declaring a technical/evidence blocker, treating negative observation as product failure, repeating a governed attempt, or escalating to the owner, the active main session performs one bounded self-diagnostic pass that classifies the affected Product Candidate, Proof Runner, Evaluator, Environment, or Authority layer; identifies current assumptions and contradictions; qualifies mandatory negative-evidence sources; and executes the smallest safe causally distinct probe when one is available. The completion guard continues unresolved autonomous diagnosis and the existing `troubleshooter` supplies one independent pre-escalation pass when main cannot resolve the cause.
- **Operating Envelope**: Loaded `opencode-kit` global authority, Material qualification guidance, diagnosis-only `troubleshooter`, and grind-enabled completion guard; local read-only inspection and contained reversible diagnostic probes; existing protected-boundary, live-attempt, writer-liveness, privacy, cleanup, and owner-authority controls; disposable same-model baseline/candidate evaluation with fixed synthetic blocker cases.
- **Non-Goals**: Exhaustively rechecking every fact on ordinary failures; adding a second troubleshooting workflow or general workflow engine; embedding network-, controller-, or `PktMon`-specific policy in portable instructions; authorizing protected/live actions; automatically changing production code or proof configuration during diagnosis; requiring a specialist for an obvious evidenced local defect; or claiming universal model compliance.
- **Non-Deferrable Invariants**: Human authority and protected boundaries remain unchanged; an unqualified observer cannot support a product-failure or owner-blocker claim; missing qualification never clears a safety, identity, liveness, authorization, data-integrity, cleanup, or live-attempt gate; another high-cost/live attempt remains governed by preserved-bundle replay; diagnostic work is finite and causally distinct; unknown remains explicit rather than becoming guessed ownership.
- **Observable Proof**: Through the installed instruction and agent route, run the same model/profile/input/environment for baseline and candidate synthetic scenarios covering a broken observer, stale machine-local identifier, wrong observation layer, zero without positive control, contradictory direct and indirect evidence, a straightforward local defect, and a true owner-only boundary. Observe that the candidate performs the permitted diagnostic probe, narrows the claim to the affected proof lane, avoids a user question and repeated costly attempt, invokes at most one correctly briefed `troubleshooter` when needed, while preserving the true owner-only result and completing cleanup.
- **Material Residual Risks**: Prompt-level reasoning can still omit or misclassify a relevant assumption; bounded synthetic scenarios cannot represent every diagnostic domain; an aggressive trigger adds latency and context at real blockers; deterministic validators can enforce structure and routing markers but cannot prove semantic diagnosis quality.
- **Stop Line**: Finish when the always-loaded trigger, Material evidence-source qualification contract, `troubleshooter` case/report contract, completion-arbiter continuation behavior, focused validator/mirror updates, and installed same-model pre-escalation evaluation are complete and proven. Do not add domain-specific diagnostic adapters, automatic production fixes, broad telemetry, unbounded hypothesis search, target-project activation, or protected external effects.

## What Changes

- Add an always-loaded self-diagnostic trigger for technical/evidence blockers, contradictory evidence, failed canaries, and zero/empty/absence-based observations.
- Require mandatory negative-evidence sources to establish current identity, observation-point relevance, freshness, and a positive control or remain explicitly unqualified.
- Require main and `troubleshooter` to classify the blocker layer, separate facts from assumptions, inspect contradictions, select one smallest falsifying probe, and preserve a claim ceiling before owner escalation.
- Make the completion arbiter continue an unresolved autonomous blocker when the self-diagnostic evidence is absent or a safe causally distinct probe remains; preserve exact owner-only boundaries without redundant consultation.
- Extend the existing pre-escalation recovery proof with disposable same-model baseline/candidate scenarios derived from the reproduced broken-observer failure class, plus structural and installed-loader validation.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `library-change-ready-sdlc`: Qualify negative evidence and require a bounded self-diagnostic pass before technical blocker or owner escalation claims.
- `session-completion-guard`: Continue autonomous diagnosis when blocker-layer, contradiction, observer-qualification, or distinct-probe evidence is missing, while retaining finite stagnation and true owner-boundary behavior.
- `library-instruction-artifacts`: Make the trigger and troubleshooter contract part of loaded portable authority and require same-model behavior evaluation for the reproduced blocker class.
