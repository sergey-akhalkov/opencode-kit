## Why

The kit can generate internally consistent plans, specifications, and architecture
artifacts that pass structural gates while a later fresh session still finds material
task-fit, scope, ownership, or proof defects. Same-session checks currently validate
the author's framing more often than they try to disprove it, while an unbounded review
loop would create speculative findings and work for its own sake.

## What Changes

- Add one bounded falsification-review contract for decision-material plans,
  specifications, architecture decisions, and Material changes before they are
  represented as semantically ready. Ordinary Small owner-local work remains exempt.
- Require authoring to start from the original accepted request, success boundary, and
  strongest realistic failure hypotheses before drafting, so the first candidate is
  shaped by likely omissions, late surprises, and unnecessary scope rather than merely
  checked after writing.
- Reuse and extend `implementation-readiness-reviewer` as the default fresh read-only
  challenger. Supply the original request independently from the authored artifacts;
  retain exact architecture, instruction, safety, claim, and other Practice Owners only
  for their existing material triggers. Do not add a universal critic or central review
  router.
- Require a Material change without a formal planning artifact to create the smallest
  decision frame needed for the same early `outcome-readiness` challenge before
  production mutation; do not introduce a mandatory late generic reviewer.
- Admit a review finding only when it identifies a violated accepted outcome or
  non-deferrable invariant, a reachable current-envelope scenario, concrete consequence,
  evidence, and the smallest correction. Speculative future behavior, style, optional
  polish, generic uncertainty, and reviewer preference create no work.
- Require the challenge to test coherent-but-wrong interpretation, silent user-owned
  decisions, unobservable acceptance, likely implementation surprise, internal
  contradiction, and unnecessary capability or task scope. Zero material findings is a
  valid successful result and finding count is never a quality measure.
- Bound one episode to one initial fresh challenge and, only after a confirmed material
  correction changes the challenged decision surface, at most one corrected-candidate
  re-review. Repeated findings on an unchanged candidate, optional findings, and a desire
  for additional confidence cannot extend the episode.
- Make main independently reproduce and disposition every material row. A reviewer does
  not authorize mutation, expand accepted scope, select product semantics, or approve
  readiness. Any confirmed accepted-outcome correction remains ordinary in-scope work.
- Distinguish deterministic artifact readiness from semantic implementation readiness in
  `/opsx-propose`. Structural OpenSpec validation and operation gates cannot imply that
  the artifacts solve the original task; the broader wording is emitted only after the
  bounded challenge is complete or a reviewed Ordinary Small exemption applies.
- Add matched configured-session scenarios and deterministic record validation for
  trigger, freshness, original-request separation, material-finding admission,
  correction invalidation, one-re-review termination, no-finding success, and Ordinary
  Small/optional-polish negative controls. Helpers validate explicit records only and do
  not score semantic quality.

### Outcome Capsule

- **Outcome:** A user receives a decision-material plan, specification, architecture
  decision, or Material-change plan that has been authored from realistic failure
  hypotheses and subjected to one honest fresh-context attempt to disprove its fit to the
  original request, with only evidence-backed material corrections applied and no
  open-ended polishing cycle.
- **Operating Envelope:** Loaded OpenCode-kit main-session behavior; OpenSpec propose and
  implementation-readiness routing; readable original user requirements and current
  artifacts; fresh read-only reviewer sessions; local project-native planning evidence;
  same-model baseline/candidate configured-session fixtures; no product implementation,
  protected action, or external mutation in the planning workflow.
- **Non-Goals:** Guaranteeing defect-free plans; reviewing every task or Ordinary Small
  edit; maximizing finding count; generic code review; exhaustive audit; replacing
  architecture, instruction, safety, evidence, or domain Practice Owners; automatic
  semantic scoring; adding a universal critic, approval authority, persistent global
  review database, or unlimited review/fix convergence loop.
- **Non-Deferrable Invariants:** The original accepted request is an independent review
  input rather than reconstructed from the candidate; fresh review evidence remains
  non-authorizing; only reachable evidence-backed material defects create correction
  work; no finding is a valid terminal result; optional and future-scope observations do
  not extend scope; Ordinary Small zero-trigger work remains direct; one episode permits
  no more than one initial challenge and one materially justified corrected-candidate
  re-review; deterministic tooling never infers semantic quality.
- **Observable Proof:** Frozen configured pre-product characterization plus current
  member-local candidate sessions show that the candidate constructs a first draft from failure hypotheses, launches
  one fresh reviewer for each reviewed decision-material partition, catches and corrects
  represented coherent-but-wrong, hidden-decision, missing-oracle, and unnecessary-scope
  defects, accepts a genuine no-material-finding result, leaves optional polish and
  Ordinary Small work untouched, performs at most one justified re-review, and never
  treats structural gates or reviewer prose as semantic authority.
- **Material Residual Risks:** Model judgment remains probabilistic and may miss or
  invent defects; the original request may itself be incomplete; fresh-context review
  adds latency and configured-model cost; trigger wording may under- or over-match until
  exercised; the reviewed partitions cannot establish universal quality across models,
  repositories, or domains; implementation overlaps active instruction-surface changes
  and must be serialized or transferred before shared files are edited.
- **Stop Line:** Finish the bounded contract, existing-owner extension, OpenSpec readiness
  distinction, compact routing, anti-loop disposition rules, structural validation, and
  matched loaded behavior evidence. Do not add another reviewer agent, all-practice
  checklist, every-task review, semantic scorer, autonomous audit campaign, review
  database, or third generic challenge in one unchanged accepted-outcome episode.

### Claim And Evidence Scope

- **Claim ID**: `bounded-falsification-review-v1`
- **Claim Class**: partitioned-domain workflow behavior
- **Population**: Reviewed configured-session partitions `coherent-wrong-outcome`,
  `silent-owner-decision`, `missing-observable-oracle`,
  `late-implementation-surprise`, `unnecessary-scope`, `clean-no-finding`,
  `material-correction-rereview`, `unchanged-repeat`, `optional-polish`,
  `ordinary-small-exempt`, `material-inline-plan`, and `exact-practice-owner`.
- **Coverage Basis**: Versioned reviewed scenario records; frozen pre-product
  characterization retained under its original identity; current candidate members use
  actor-visible member-local initial state plus recorded model, profile, permissions,
  environment, original request, and candidate source identity; configured root/child
  captures; exact tool/session attribution; artifact and reviewer record readback; one
  terminal row per represented partition. A baseline/candidate comparison is direct only
  when every compared identity matches; historical characterization otherwise remains a
  separate lane and supports no causal improvement claim.
- **Production Path**: Loaded primary main-session authoring and OpenSpec propose routing,
  fresh `implementation-readiness-reviewer` challenge, main-owned reproduction and
  correction, and at most one corrected-candidate re-review.
- **Comparison Paths**: Frozen pre-product characterization versus current candidate
  instructions as separately identified lanes; candidate artifact alone versus
  original-request-plus-artifact challenge; material finding versus
  no-finding, optional-polish, Ordinary Small, repeated-unchanged, and exact-owner routes.
- **Environment**: Current configured OpenCode model/profile and local disposable
  repositories with no consumer-product, credential, remote, deployment, destructive, or
  protected effect.
- **Real Oracle**: Actual configured main and fresh-child behavior, tool/session routing,
  generated artifact differences, main disposition, re-review count, and terminal
  handoff inspected after writer/session closure; deterministic checks are structural
  support only.
- **Unresolved Observations**: Other models/providers and prompt histories; incomplete or
  contradictory original requests; domains absent from the reviewed partition set;
  long-term defect-rate and latency effects; non-OpenSpec planning systems without a
  durable artifact; interactions with active unimplemented foundation-integrity and
  autonomous-campaign changes.
- **Maximum Claim**: For the exercised configured model and reviewed generic partitions,
  the candidate performs one bounded fresh falsification episode, admits only represented
  material findings, preserves negative controls, and terminates without a generic third
  challenge. It does not prove causal improvement over the historically characterized
  source, defect-free planning, universal reviewer judgment, or quality improvement for
  unreviewed models, domains, and repositories.

- **Automation Dividend**: required - extend the existing consumer-outcome configured-
  session harness with a bounded-falsification partition pack and deterministic schema,
  identity, count, freshness, and terminal-state checks while keeping all semantic
  findings and expected dispositions in reviewed scenario records.
- **Bounded Falsification Review**: required - decision-material authored artifacts and Material inline decision frames before semantic implementation readiness

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `library-bounded-falsification-review`: Define decision-material triggers, original-
  request-grounded challenge, material finding admission, main disposition, freshness,
  invalidation, and finite termination.

### Modified Capabilities

- `library-instruction-artifacts`: Require falsification-before-confidence for
  decision-material authored results and extend the existing readiness reviewer contract
  without creating a universal critic.
- `library-spec-workflow-integrity`: Distinguish structural propose readiness from
  semantic implementation readiness and integrate the bounded review/exemption path.
- `library-practice-ownership`: Extend the `outcome-readiness` trigger and reviewed owner
  semantics while preserving exact-owner routing and zero-trigger Ordinary Small work.
- `library-change-ready-sdlc`: Route one pre-investment falsification episode for
  decision-material and Material planning, with no duplicate generic post-proof review
  unless the challenged decision surface materially changes.

## Impact

- Loaded instruction behavior in `global/principles-of-work.md`, `global/AGENTS.md`, the
  readiness reviewer, OpenSpec propose command/skill mirrors, and proportional review
  routing. Running sessions require restart after later implementation is installed.
- Practice ownership registry semantics, reviewed generated views, profiles/catalogs,
  validators, README routing, and focused contract tests may require synchronized edits;
  no new Practice Owner or profile member is intended.
- Existing consumer-outcome proof fixtures and evaluator gain a bounded review pack; no
  new semantic helper, dependency, public product API, protocol, or persisted product
  format is introduced.
- Shared instruction/practice/proof paths overlap active changes, especially
  `add-foundation-integrity-autorecovery` and `add-continuous-complexity-management`;
  implementation must establish path-level serialization, dependency order, or explicit
  ownership transfer before mutation.
