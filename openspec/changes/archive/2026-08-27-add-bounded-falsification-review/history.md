## 2026-08-26 - Rely on stronger same-context self-review

- **Objective:** Improve first-pass quality without adding another session or reviewer.
- **Approach:** Add more provocative questions to the authoring prompt and let the same
  author context review its completed artifact.
- **Evidence:** The user's repeated observation is that same-session review misses
  significant issues later found by a fresh session. The inspected `/opsx-propose` path
  already creates coherent artifacts and validates them inside the authoring context but
  performs no independent semantic challenge.
- **Outcome:** Rejected as the sole mechanism; retained only as pre-authoring failure
  framing to improve the first candidate.
- **Reason:** It lowers avoidable defects but does not remove framing continuity or
  provide independent evidence.
- **Do-Not-Repeat Condition:** Do not represent author-side inversion or self-review as a
  fresh challenge.
- **Evidence-Based Retry Condition:** Reconsider independence only if matched configured
  evidence shows same-context review detects the reviewed defect partitions as reliably
  as a fresh child with lower total cost and no weaker negative controls.
