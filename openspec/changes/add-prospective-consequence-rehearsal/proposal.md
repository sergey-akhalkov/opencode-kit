## Why

Decision-material reviews currently see the proposed mechanism before they reconstruct the actors, state, lifecycle, ownership, and proof boundaries that the mechanism must preserve. A locally coherent candidate can therefore collapse a consequential distinction while the review merely checks the candidate's own frame; the maintained regression pack reinforces that bias by supplying the represented risk instead of testing whether the reviewer discovers it.
