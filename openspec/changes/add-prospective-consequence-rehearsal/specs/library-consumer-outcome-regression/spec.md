## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Answer-free packs test contextual distinction discovery

The consumer-outcome regression capability SHALL accept one versioned `prospective-consequence-rehearsal` focused decision-gap pack that reuses the maintained matched source staging, configured capture, environment correlation, hard outcome and safety gates, privacy bounds, cleanup, and provider-free evaluator. The pack SHALL remain separate from the general scenarios and every existing focused pack and SHALL use generic disposable repositories with one sample per baseline and candidate arm.

The reviewed pack SHALL cover the finite PCR-001 population: unlabeled actor, state/lifecycle, and ownership/recovery distinctions; a Material-without-plan inline decision whose frame is created after reconstruction; insufficient evidence; candidate materialized before reconstruction as a red control; unverified continuation; corrected-candidate freshness and stale reconstruction; Ordinary Small exact work; and exact skip/omit/substitution routing. Actor-visible phase-one inputs SHALL contain only original outcome/envelope facts and raw system evidence. The current file-candidate path/content and inline-frame content SHALL not exist in the disposable project or root prompt until reconstruction returns. Phase-one prompt, files, and tool results SHALL therefore contain no `representedRisk`, corrected artifact or answer, expected consequence labels, candidate bytes, or mechanism-revealing candidate summary. Capture SHALL materialize the file or inline-frame candidate only before the second exact child continuation. Expected observations SHALL remain evaluator-only reviewed data outside every model-visible fixture and prompt.

Deterministic preflight SHALL validate stable scenario order, temporal candidate-materialization plan, forbidden-field absence, contained paths, expected-observation completeness, explicit provider/tool bounds, and maximum claim without inferring semantic distinctions. Capture SHALL record the pre-reconstruction filesystem manifest, all stage-one task/tool inputs and outputs, post-reconstruction candidate creation, exact child continuation, and corrected-review identity. Evaluation SHALL inspect those facts, reconstruction/comparison outputs, honest `unknown`, route selection, forbidden effects, and cleanup. It SHALL use explicit expected protocol observations and one reviewed candidate semantic-containment mode per scenario: `required-consequence`, `required-unknown`, `evidence-bounded`, or `not-applicable`. `Evidence-bounded` SHALL accept only either a closed result with at least one consequence and no unknown reason, or an unknown result with no consequence and an explicit reason; it SHALL reject mixed and empty states. Required-unknown controls SHALL still reject fabricated consequences. Natural-language consequence identifiers SHALL remain available for manual evidence inspection but SHALL NOT be compared to hidden evaluator tokens or used to claim discovery reliability from one sample per arm. The intentional extra reviewer calls SHALL be explicit pack oracles and SHALL not be treated as general friction regressions.

#### Scenario: Pack reconstructs before candidate creation

- **WHEN** capture starts the prospective-consequence reconstruction
- **THEN** the pre-reconstruction filesystem manifest contains no current candidate path or content sentinel and all stage-one model-visible inputs/results are free of candidate bytes, `representedRisk`, corrected answers, and expected consequence labels
- **AND** only after reconstruction does capture materialize the candidate for exact continuation while expectations remain evaluator-only.

#### Scenario: Candidate discovers an unlabeled distinction

- **WHEN** raw evidence contains one reviewed consequential distinction and the candidate arm uses candidate-separated reconstruction and exact comparison
- **THEN** evaluation observes the expected evidence-backed distinction row without the model having received the risk label
- **AND** the claim remains limited to that scenario, model, source, fixture, and environment identity.

#### Scenario: Inline decision frame follows reconstruction

- **WHEN** a Material-without-plan scenario has distinction pressure and no decision frame exists at stage-one launch
- **THEN** the frame sentinel is absent from every stage-one prompt/file/tool result, capture creates the frame only after reconstruction, and the exact child continuation compares it as the candidate
- **AND** a create-frame-then-review ordering fails the scenario even when every file-candidate scenario passes.

#### Scenario: Insufficient evidence remains unknown

- **WHEN** the raw evidence cannot establish a downstream consumer or exact reviewer continuity
- **THEN** the accepted observation is `unknown` with the missing fact rather than a fabricated consequence or a closed challenge
- **AND** another scenario's success cannot compensate for that result.

#### Scenario: Candidate is created before reconstruction

- **WHEN** the candidate path exists before reconstruction completes or capture observes candidate bytes, an author-named risk, corrected answer, or expected consequence label in any stage-one prompt, file, or tool result
- **THEN** evaluation fails the exact arm, scenario, sample, and leakage oracle
- **AND** fluent reviewer output cannot compensate for the invalid separation.

#### Scenario: Corrected candidate uses current frozen reconstruction

- **WHEN** one confirmed correction changes the candidate but not the raw context represented by the pre-authoring reconstruction
- **THEN** a fresh reviewer compares the corrected candidate against the same reconstruction and challenge count remains two
- **AND** a stale reconstruction yields `unknown` rather than another candidate-visible reconstruction.

#### Scenario: Proportional controls retain their owners

- **WHEN** the pack runs its Ordinary Small exact and skip/omit/substitution controls
- **THEN** Ordinary Small adds no readiness reviewer route and the substitution control uses `behavioral-substitution-qualification` without a duplicate prospective rehearsal
- **AND** existing proof, safety, authorization, and cleanup behavior remains unchanged.

#### Scenario: Pack evidence cannot alter another pack or baseline

- **WHEN** every prospective-consequence scenario passes
- **THEN** the maintained general baseline, friction expectation, and every other focused pack remain byte-unchanged
- **AND** no universal foresight, productivity, or untested-project claim is emitted.
