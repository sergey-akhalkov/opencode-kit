## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Implementation readiness review exposes discarded consequential distinctions

The kit SHALL extend the existing `implementation-readiness-reviewer` rather than add a sibling universal critic. Its role-specific contract SHALL support a context-reconstruction response completed before candidate authoring and an exact-session resumed initial candidate-comparison response. The reconstruction SHALL expose current actors or consumers, state and lifecycle, ownership and recovery, observable distinctions, causal-use boundaries, and evidence gaps. The comparison SHALL emit evidence-backed consequence rows without an acceptance verdict, lifecycle blocker, user question, nested agent, source mutation, or finding-count target. A fresh corrected-candidate reviewer SHALL consume the still-current frozen reconstruction rather than repeat reconstruction around an existing candidate.

The agent and loaded orchestration surfaces SHALL keep phase-one forbidden inputs, phase-two exact continuation, corrected-candidate freshness, main disposition authority, and the `no-material-finding` outcome explicit and easy to retrieve. Structural validators SHALL check only stable role, permission, section, and protocol markers; configured-session evidence SHALL establish candidate separation and resulting behavior.

#### Scenario: Reviewer receives a pre-authoring reconstruction brief

- **WHEN** a fresh readiness reviewer is invoked before the first candidate artifact, inline frame, or source mutation exists
- **THEN** its prompt, files, and tool results contain no current candidate bytes, author-named represented risk, corrected answer, or mechanism-revealing summary
- **AND** its output remains bounded to source-supported context and unknowns.

#### Scenario: Same reviewer receives the candidate

- **WHEN** main resumes the exact reviewer child after reconstruction
- **THEN** the reviewer compares the supplied candidate to its prior reconstruction and returns consequence rows or `no-material-finding`
- **AND** it does not emit an approval, rejection, lifecycle verdict, or mutation instruction.

#### Scenario: Corrected candidate receives fresh comparison

- **WHEN** a confirmed correction changes a challenged surface while the frozen pre-authoring reconstruction remains current
- **THEN** a new fresh reviewer compares the corrected candidate against that reconstruction without repeating candidate-visible context reconstruction
- **AND** an invalidated reconstruction leaves the generic re-review unknown.

#### Scenario: Structural markers exist without loaded proof

- **WHEN** agent validators find every candidate-separated protocol marker but no matched configured-session evidence exists
- **THEN** structural validation passes only the artifact contract
- **AND** instruction-effectiveness readiness remains `unknown`.
