## Why

The kit describes Ordinary Small as the default direct route, but current loaded routing requires a specialist-team-advisor decision for most new non-trivial roots and current OpenSpec authoring requires several explicit no-op declarations even for an exact local change. Exact prose markers then make policy wording itself a validation boundary, so routine closure pays model, artifact, synchronization, and retry costs that do not protect the accepted outcome.

### Outcome Capsule

- **Outcome**: A clear bounded Ordinary Small change follows main-owned implementation, representative runtime proof, and focused validation without mandatory team advice, no-op OpenSpec declarations, or exact prose-mirror synchronization; Material and exact triggered owner routes retain their current safeguards.
- **Operating Envelope**: The installed kit's loaded primary instructions, maintained project instruction surfaces, OpenSpec propose/apply operation gates for new changes, and their focused validators and consumer-outcome fixtures. Existing active and archived changes remain readable under their recorded contracts.
- **Non-Goals**: Evidence-directory cleanup; campaign wave policy; exhaustive-audit ledger policy; general CI optimization; removal of the advisor, Practice Owners, OpenSpec, runtime proof, task-completeness archive checks, or protected-boundary controls; implementation or archival disposition of other active changes.
- **Non-Deferrable Invariants**: No task may be downgraded from Material based on size; registered Practice Owners still launch on their exact material triggers; owner, authorization, privacy, data-integrity, destructive-action, deployment, and other protected boundaries remain unchanged; deterministic tooling never infers semantic risk from prose; missing or invalid new profile metadata fails closed to the legacy/Material path; existing proposal records retain compatible validation.
- **Observable Proof**: Through the actual installed OpenCode entry point, matched disposable scenarios show a bounded Ordinary Small root completing with no advisor call and an advisor-value positive control still consulting the advisor. Through the production OpenSpec gate, a new Ordinary Small fixture passes with the minimal profile and artifacts while a Material fixture, an invalid profile, a triggered Practice Owner case, and representative legacy changes retain their stricter current behavior. Focused validators reject reintroduced mandatory no-op records and exact full-sentence mirror coupling.
- **Material Residual Risks**: Model-owned Ordinary Small versus Material classification can still be wrong; a structured profile can be misdeclared; removing exact phrase checks can hide instruction drift unless replacement behavior or structural oracles are strong; mixed old/new OpenSpec records increase parser branches; active instruction changes currently overlap several planned write roots.
- **Stop Line**: Finish one direct Ordinary Small routing contract, one backward-compatible structured OpenSpec profile with a minimal Ordinary Small artifact contract, removal of only the superseded advisor/compaction and no-op declaration mirrors, focused structural and installed behavior proof, and current ownership reconciliation. Stop before changing campaign, audit-ledger, evidence retention, unrelated reviewer routing, general CI topology, qualification semantics, or consumer-project behavior.
- **Delivery Horizon:** none - this is a local workflow-friction correction with no declared project delivery Horizon.
