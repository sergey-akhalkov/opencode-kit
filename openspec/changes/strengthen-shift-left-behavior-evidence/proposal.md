## Why

The kit requires shift-left sequencing and deterministically protects its wording, but the maintained loaded-model evidence does not directly challenge whether an agent places an already reachable sufficient real characterization before dependent implementation or avoids an unnecessary higher-fidelity rung. This leaves a semantic evidence gap between instruction presence and observed planning behavior.

## Outcome Capsule

- **Outcome**: The maintained matched consumer-outcome proof can evaluate two fixed shift-left decisions against loaded kit instructions: characterize an already reachable sufficient real boundary before dependent expansion, and stop at a lower sufficient rung even when a higher protected rung is available.
- **Operating Envelope**: One versioned focused decision-gap pack, generic disposable repositories, the existing `quality-independent` route, one fixed non-sensitive request per scenario and arm, explicit baseline/candidate source identities, bounded configured-provider calls, provider-free replay, local proof-owned writes, and no target-project or external effects.
- **Non-Goals**: Add a sixth working principle, rewrite `principles-of-work.md`, alter the general consumer baseline or friction claim, guarantee compliance across models or arbitrary projects, score prose quality, measure elapsed time, require a universal reviewer/SDET gate, or authorize credentials, installation, activation, deployment, release, remote, destructive, costly, or physical action.
- **Non-Deferrable Invariants**: Baseline and candidate use identical prompt, model, variant, OpenCode version, permission envelope, fixture state, and evaluator semantics; the focused pack remains unable to replace or promote the general baseline; a lower-rung result cannot claim an unobserved higher rung; protected authority remains separate; critical observations, source identity, cleanup, or environment mismatches fail closed; no deterministic helper infers semantic correctness from marker presence.
- **Observable Proof**: Freeze current loaded-source baseline responses, run the same two scenarios against the readable candidate through the configured installed OpenCode boundary, preserve exact decision artifacts and environment identities, and replay the bundles provider-free to show both candidate decisions pass without weaker safety, cleanup, or claim-ceiling facts.
- **Material Residual Risks**: Two synthetic scenarios and one configured route cannot establish universal model compliance; configured inference is nondeterministic; a passing decision artifact does not prove later execution follows the plan; and a source correction may affect instruction interpretation outside the bounded pack.
- **Stop Line**: Finish when the separate focused pack, deterministic schemas/oracles, matched capture/replay path, focused tests, proof inventory, delta specs, and current-candidate evidence are complete. If frozen baseline exposes a shift-left defect, correct only the smallest canonical instruction owner and required mirrors, then recapture the affected candidate lane; do not add another runner, general productivity scenario, lifecycle stage, reviewer gate, or unrelated instruction cleanup.
- **Automation Dividend**: required - extend the existing consumer-outcome regression manifest, capture, evaluator, replay, and focused-test owners with one deterministic shift-left decision pack rather than maintaining ad hoc prompts or a second proof runner.

## Claim And Evidence Scope

- **Claim ID**: `SLBE-001`
- **Claim Class**: Bounded matched instruction-behavior comparison.
- **Population**: Exactly the two reviewed shift-left scenarios, one selected configured model/profile, one explicit baseline source, and one readable candidate source.
- **Coverage Basis**: One fixed sample per scenario and arm with complete environment correlation, hard decision/safety/cleanup oracles, and provider-free replay.
- **Production Path**: Fresh installed OpenCode primary loading the candidate kit source in a disposable generic repository.
- **Comparison Paths**: Fresh installed OpenCode primary loading the frozen baseline source and the same provider-free evaluator replaying both immutable bundles.
- **Environment**: Recorded OpenCode, Node/Bun, operating-system class, model, variant, profile, config-source, fixture, prompt, permission, runner, evaluator, and manifest identities.
- **Real Oracle**: Exact plan artifact and command observations show characterization precedes dependent work in the first scenario and the lower sufficient rung is selected without protected execution in the second; source, effects, validation, and cleanup facts remain complete.
- **Unresolved Observations**: Generalization to other models, prompts, repositories, longer task graphs, and whether a produced plan is later executed faithfully.
- **Maximum Claim**: The captured candidate satisfies the two fixed shift-left decisions under the recorded environment; no universal adherence, execution, productivity, safety, or higher-rung claim is supported.

## What Changes

- Add a separate reviewed `shift-left` focused decision-gap pack to the existing consumer-outcome regression mechanism without changing its maintained general scenarios, accepted baseline pointer, friction expectation, or claim-evidence pack.
- Add deterministic preflight, schema, evaluator, replay, privacy, cleanup, and mismatch oracles for the two shift-left decisions.
- Require matched configured baseline/candidate evidence through the installed loaded-instruction boundary; retain provider-free fixtures for negative-oracle and replay validation.
- Correct loaded instruction wording only if the frozen baseline demonstrates a reproducible semantic defect, and only at the canonical owner plus required maintained mirrors.
- Update the maintained proof inventory and requirement traceability with the focused pack's exact effects, evidence, cleanup, and maximum claim.
