## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Material dependency chains use evidence-bearing leaves
For Material work, main SHALL decompose each compound behavior dependency chain into the smallest useful leaves before its next costly, live, or integration action. Each leaf SHALL identify its bounded result, owning boundary, earliest sufficient real oracle, dependency-chain stop condition, relevant authority and safeguards, restoration or cleanup delta, and expected evidence. Common change-level envelope facts SHALL remain shared and SHALL NOT be repeated in every leaf.

Main SHALL climb the fidelity ladder only after the current leaf's required oracle passes. A newly exposed independent prerequisite SHALL become a child and block only its dependent cone; a same-leaf local defect SHALL remain ordinary correction; an integration-only failure SHALL remain at the parent boundary. Leaf decomposition SHALL NOT weaken live-attempt replay, writer-liveness, protected-action, evidence population, substitution, or critical-risk controls.

#### Scenario: Material plan has multiple independent unknowns
- **WHEN** one planned behavior slice spans independently failing environment, runner, and product prerequisites before a live integration boundary
- **THEN** main represents and proves those prerequisites as dependency-bearing leaves at their earliest sufficient boundaries
- **AND** does not diagnose them together through the live parent action.

#### Scenario: Leaf correction preserves evidence topology
- **WHEN** a leaf correction changes only the Proof Runner and not the Product Candidate or trustworthy raw observations
- **THEN** invalidation remains limited to dependent runner captures and derived results
- **AND** unrelated leaf and product evidence remains current under existing Change-Ready rules.

#### Scenario: Protected action blocks one leaf
- **WHEN** one required leaf needs a protected action and another accepted leaf is dependency-valid and authorized
- **THEN** main preserves the protected leaf's exact gate and continues the independent leaf
- **AND** decomposition neither grants the action nor globally blocks the accepted outcome.
