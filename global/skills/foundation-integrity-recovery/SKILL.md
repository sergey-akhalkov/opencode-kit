# Foundation Integrity Recovery

Main-owned procedure for one reproduced current foundation defect. This skill is not an agent, reviewer, semantic classifier, or second orchestrator. Reviewer evidence never authorizes mutation.

## When To Load

Load only after main independently reproduces a `foundation-integrity` finding that is current, materially reachable, foundation-level, and capable of affecting the accepted outcome or a non-deferrable invariant.

Stay unloaded when the finding is falsified; the work is an Ordinary Small exact case; evidence is explicitly historical; the relation is aligned; or the observation is optional architecture, maintainability, test polish, future scope, or unrelated debt.

## Required Inputs

- Original accepted outcome and enforced operating envelope.
- Exact candidate and current workload/population, profile/configuration, environment, and oracle relation.
- Reviewer finding plus main's independent reproduction or falsification evidence.
- Current project-native planning, specification, claim, active-change, and archive surfaces.
- Protected boundaries, active write ownership, proof lanes, and truthful current evidence ceilings.

## Admission And Incident

1. Falsify the exact contradiction against the strongest current source or smallest safe discriminating runtime observation before semantic mutation.
2. If falsified, record the conflicting evidence and terminal state `falsified`; create no recovery work and do not seek an equivalent review for the unchanged tuple.
3. If safe evidence cannot resolve a protected product, public API, persisted-data, security, privacy, authorization, legal, or policy identity, record terminal state `owner-boundary`. Stop only the dependent identity choice after exhausting safe discrimination; continue unrelated work.
4. Otherwise bind one stable Foundation Incident ID to the exact relation, candidate, and decision-changing evidence. Use one episode only: `observed -> confirmed -> correcting -> swept -> re-reviewed -> closed`.

Deterministic tooling may validate explicit IDs, states, references, and hashes. It must not infer materiality, dependency, correction, or closure.

## Serial Recovery

1. Preserve the original accepted outcome and select the smallest uniquely determined current foundation correction.
2. Inventory every active change, including complete but unarchived changes, every current canonical specification, and every active structured claim record before mutation.
3. Classify each artifact explicitly as `not-dependent | dependent-rebind | dependent-narrow | owner-boundary | unknown`. A historical-only mention is not dependence.
4. Preserve archive and immutable-evidence hashes before writes. Never rewrite or delete archived artifacts.
5. Update only `dependent-rebind` and `dependent-narrow` artifacts. Serialize every write; use existing dependency and ownership-transfer rules for overlapping surfaces. Change canonical OpenSpec behavior through one owning active delta and normal synchronization, never through an ad hoc main-spec edit.
6. Reopen or add only tasks required by the corrected accepted outcome. Preserve prior proof at its strongest truthful historical, cross-profile, component, regression, or exact-path ceiling.
7. Re-prove every candidate lane invalidated by the correction and retain unaffected trustworthy evidence.
8. After the complete dependent sweep reaches `swept`, immediately call at most one fresh `foundation-integrity-reviewer` for the corrected candidate. Do not narrate a pending call or repeat main-only readback first; the fresh review must directly inspect the corrected and preserved artifacts. When it returns `no-material-finding`, immediately record the required terminal recovery result without another main-only readback or narrated pending write, then close the incident.

## Anti-Loop Boundary

- The same finding, candidate, relation, and evidence tuple cannot open a second incident, successor change, or equivalent verdict-seeking review.
- A remaining reproduced defect is corrected inside the current episode.
- A closed incident can reopen only for a new candidate identity or new decision-changing evidence establishing a distinct current relation.
- Non-critical or optional observations from corrected-candidate review create no change, task, backlog, review loop, or completion gate.
- A narrated owner, skill, or tool invocation that was not actually called is no evidence and cannot advance incident state.

## Output

Return main's bounded recovery record:

- Foundation Incident ID, exact candidate, relation, and reproduction evidence.
- State transitions and terminal state.
- Complete active-artifact inventory with one disposition per item.
- Corrected and intentionally unchanged artifacts.
- Prior and narrowed evidence ceilings plus archive/unrelated readback.
- Corrected-candidate proof and one fresh re-review identity, or exact reason no review was applicable.
- Remaining protected owner boundary, material unknown, and known non-critical limitations, or `none`.
- Original accepted outcome continuation point.

Never report universal foundation correctness, choose unresolved protected semantics, recurse into this skill, authorize an external action, or treat a reviewer report as completion authority.
